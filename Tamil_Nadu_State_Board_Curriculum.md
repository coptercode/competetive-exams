# Tamil Nadu State Board Curriculum (Classes 9-12)

## Class 12

### Mathematics Volume 1

*   **Chapter 1: Applications of Matrices and Determinants**
    *   Adjoint and Inverse: Adjoint calculation, properties of inverse, solving systems.
    *   Rank of Matrix: Row echelon form, rank evaluation, consistency tests.
    *   Cramer's & Gauss Rules: Determinants method, gaussian elimination back-substitution.
*   **Chapter 2: Complex Numbers**
    *   Complex Algebra: Real/imaginary components, operations, geometric representation.
    *   Polar and Euler Form: Modulus, principal argument, polar coordinate mapping.
    *   De Moivre Theorem: Powers and roots of complex numbers, nth roots of unity.
*   **Chapter 3: Theory of Equations**
    *   Vieta's Formulas: Relation between roots and coefficients of polynomials.
    *   Roots analysis: Descartes rule of signs, rational and complex roots.
*   **Chapter 4: Inverse Trigonometric Functions**
    *   Inverse Ratios: Domain, range and principal values of arcsin, arccos, arctan.
    *   Trigonometric Identities: Sum/difference formulas, properties of inverse functions.
*   **Chapter 5: Two-Dimensional Analytical Geometry-II**
    *   Conics Classification: Standard equations of parabola, ellipse, hyperbola.
    *   Tangents & Normals: Equations of tangent and normal to conics at a point.
*   **Chapter 6: Vector Algebra**
    *   Scalar & Vector Triple Products: Definition of triple products, volume of parallelepiped.
    *   Planes & Lines: Vector and Cartesian equations of lines and planes in 3D.

### Mathematics Volume 2

*   **Chapter 7: Applications of Differential Calculus**
    *   Mean Value Theorems: Rolle's theorem, Lagrange's mean value theorem.
    *   Extrema: Local maxima/minima, concavity, points of inflection.
*   **Chapter 8: Differentials and Partial Derivatives**
    *   Linear Approximation: Differentials, error calculations, linear approximations.
    *   Partial Derivatives: Euler's theorem on homogeneous functions, chain rule.
*   **Chapter 9: Applications of Integration**
    *   Definite Integral Properties: Evaluating standard definite integrals, properties.
    *   Area and Volume: Area bounded by curves, volume of solid of revolution.
*   **Chapter 10: Ordinary Differential Equations**
    *   Formation of ODEs: Order, degree, forming differential equations.
    *   Solving Methods: Variable separable, homogeneous, linear first-order ODEs.
*   **Chapter 11: Probability Distributions**
    *   Random Variables: Discrete and continuous random variables, probability mass/density.
    *   Standard Distributions: Binomial distribution, normal distribution properties.
*   **Chapter 12: Discrete Mathematics**
    *   Binary Operations: Properties of operations (associative, identity, inverse).
    *   Mathematical Logic: Truth tables, tautologies, logical equivalences.

### Physics Volume 1

*   **Unit 1: Electrostatics**
    *   Coulomb's Law: Electric charge, electric force, superposition principle.
    *   Electric Field & Potential: Electric dipole, electric potential, Gauss's law applications.
    *   Capacitors: Capacitance, energy in capacitor, dielectrics behavior.
*   **Unit 2: Current Electricity**
    *   Electric Current: Drift velocity, ohm's law, temperature coefficient.
    *   Kirchhoff's Laws: Wheatstone bridge, meter bridge, potentiometer.
    *   Electric Power: Joule's heating law, thermoelectric effects (Seebeck).
*   **Unit 3: Magnetism and Magnetic Effects**
    *   Bar Magnet: Magnetic field, torque, magnetic properties (dia/para/ferro).
    *   Biot-Savart Law: Ampere's law, magnetic field of current loop and solenoid.
    *   Lorentz Force: Force on current-carrying conductor, galvanometer conversion.
*   **Unit 4: Electromagnetic Induction and Alternating Current**
    *   Faraday's Laws: Electromagnetic induction, Lenz's law, self/mutual induction.
    *   AC Generator: Alternating currents, RMS value, LCR series circuit.
    *   Transformer: Working principle, energy losses in a transformer.
*   **Unit 5: Electromagnetic Waves**
    *   Displacement Current: Maxwell's equations modification, electromagnetic spectrum.
    *   Spectrum Properties: Radio, micro, infrared, visible, UV, X-rays, gamma properties.

### Physics Volume 2

*   **Unit 6: Ray Optics**
    *   Refraction & Reflection: Mirror equation, refractive index, total internal reflection.
    *   Prisms & Lenses: Lens maker's formula, angle of minimum deviation in prism.
*   **Unit 7: Wave Optics**
    *   Interference: Young's double slit experiment, fringe width expression.
    *   Diffraction & Polarization: Single slit diffraction, Brewster's law, polaroids.
*   **Unit 8: Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter**
    *   Photoelectric Effect: Hertz-Hallwachs observations, Einstein's explanation.
    *   Matter Waves: De Broglie wavelength, Davisson-Germer experiment.
*   **Unit 9: Atomic and Nuclear Physics**
    *   Atom Models: Rutherford and Bohr models, hydrogen spectrum series.
    *   Nuclear Structure: Mass defect, binding energy curve, fission, fusion.
*   **Unit 10: Electronics and Communication**
    *   Semiconductors: P-N junction diode, Zener diode, half/full wave rectifiers.
    *   Logic Gates: AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR truth tables, de Morgan's proofs.
*   **Unit 11: Recent Developments in Physics**
    *   Nanotechnology: Nanoscales, quantum dots, carbon nanotubes, applications.

### Chemistry Volume 1

*   **Unit 1: Metallurgy**
    *   Concentration of Ores: Gravity separation, froth floatation, electromagnetic separation.
    *   Extraction of Crude Metal: Roasting, calcination, reduction of metal oxides.
    *   Refining Processes: Zone refining, Mond process, Van Arkel method.
*   **Unit 2: p-Block Elements - I**
    *   Group 13 Elements: Boron family general properties, diborane, borax.
    *   Group 14 Elements: Carbon family allotropes, silicates classification.
*   **Unit 3: p-Block Elements - II**
    *   Nitrogen & Phosphorus: Group 15 elements, ammonia, nitric acid, phosphine.
    *   Oxygen & Halogens: Sulfuric acid, chlorine, noble gases compounds.
*   **Unit 4: Transition and Inner Transition Elements**
    *   d-Block Elements: General configuration, metallic character, interstitial compounds.
    *   Lanthanides & Actinides: Lanthanide contraction causes and consequences, differences.
*   **Unit 5: Coordination Chemistry**
    *   Terminology: Double salts vs coordination compounds, Werner's theory.
    *   Nomenclature & Valence Bond: IUPAC naming of complexes, VB theory predictions.
*   **Unit 6: Solid State**
    *   Crystalline Solids: Classification, unit cell, packing efficiency in cubic.
    *   Crystal Defects: Stoichiometric defects (Schottky, Frenkel), non-stoichiometric.
*   **Unit 7: Chemical Kinetics**
    *   Rate of Reaction: Average and instantaneous rates, order vs molecularity.
    *   Integrated Rate Equations: First-order reaction, half-life period, Arrhenius equation.

### Chemistry Volume 2

*   **Unit 8: Ionic Equilibrium**
    *   Acids and Bases: Arrhenius, Bronsted-Lowry, Lewis theories, pH/pOH calculation.
    *   Buffer Solutions: Henderson-Hasselbalch equation, solubility product Ksp.
*   **Unit 9: Electrochemistry**
    *   Conductivity: Specific and molar conductance, Kohlrausch's law.
    *   Galvanic Cells: Nernst equation, standard hydrogen electrode SHE.
*   **Unit 10: Surface Chemistry**
    *   Adsorption: Physisorption vs chemisorption, Freundlich isotherm.
    *   Colloids & Catalysis: Tyndall effect, electrophoresis, homogeneous catalysis.
*   **Unit 11: Hydroxy Compounds and Ethers**
    *   Alcohols Preparation: Grignard synthesis, Lucas test, Victor Meyer test.
    *   Phenols & Ethers: Preparation of phenol, acidity, Williamson synthesis.
*   **Unit 12: Carbonyl Compounds and Carboxylic Acids**
    *   Aldehydes & Ketones: Nucleophilic addition, aldol condensation, Cannizzaro.
    *   Carboxylic Acids: Acidity of carboxylic acids, HVZ reaction.
*   **Unit 13: Organic Nitrogen Compounds**
    *   Amines: Classification, Hoffmann bromamide reaction, basicity.
    *   Diazonium Salts: Preparation of aniline, coupling reactions.
*   **Unit 14: Biomolecules**
    *   Carbohydrates: Classification, structure of glucose and fructose.
    *   Proteins & Nucleic Acids: Amino acids, peptide bond, double helix DNA structure.
*   **Unit 15: Chemistry in Everyday Life**
    *   Drugs & Polymers: Antiseptics, analgesics, addition/condensation polymers.

## Class 11

### Mathematics Volume 1

*   **Chapter 1: Sets, Relations and Functions**
    *   Sets Operations: Set operations, subsets, power sets, Cartesian product.
    *   Relations and Equivalence: Types of relations, equivalence relation definition, partition.
    *   Functions: Types of functions, injection, surjection, bijection, composition.
*   **Chapter 2: Basic Algebra**
    *   Real Numbers: Rational/irrational numbers, inequalities, absolute value.
    *   Quadratic Equations: Roots of quadratic equations, sum and product, sign behavior.
    *   Partial Fractions & Logarithms: Decomposition rules, logarithm laws, solving equations.
*   **Chapter 3: Trigonometry**
    *   Trigonometric Functions: Angle measurement, radian and degree, circular functions.
    *   Compound Angles: Addition and subtraction formulas, double/half angle formulas.
    *   Trigonometric Equations: General solutions of equations, properties of triangles.
*   **Chapter 4: Combinatorics and Mathematical Induction**
    *   Principles of Counting: Addition and multiplication principles, factorial notation.
    *   Permutations and Combinations: Derivation of nPr and nCr, simple arrangements, selections.
    *   Mathematical Induction: Induction principle, proving sum and divisibility formulas.
*   **Chapter 5: Binomial Theorem, Sequences and Series**
    *   Binomial Theorem: Expansion for positive integral index, general and middle terms.
    *   Arithmetic and Geometric Progressions: General term, sum of n terms of AP/GP, arithmetic-geometric series.
    *   Infinite Series: Infinite GP sum, binomial series expansions, simple approximations.
*   **Chapter 6: Two Dimensional Analytical Geometry**
    *   Locus of a Point: Definition of locus, finding equation of a locus.
    *   Straight Lines: Various equations forms, angle between lines, distance formula.
    *   Pair of Straight Lines: Homogeneous equation of second degree, angle between them.

### Mathematics Volume 2

*   **Chapter 7: Matrices and Determinants**
    *   Matrix Algebra: Types of matrices, operations, transpose, symmetric matrices.
    *   Determinants: Evaluation of determinants, properties, area of a triangle.
    *   Singular and Non-Singular: Adjoint, inverse of 2x2 and 3x3 matrices, systems of equations.
*   **Chapter 8: Vector Algebra**
    *   Vectors Representation: Types of vectors, addition, section formula, direction cosines.
    *   Scalar Product: Dot product definition, projection of a vector, work done.
    *   Vector Product: Cross product, torque vector representation, area of triangle.
*   **Chapter 9: Differential Calculus - Limits and Continuity**
    *   Limits Concept: One-sided limits, limit laws, standard algebraic limits.
    *   Trigonometric Limits: Limits of trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions.
    *   Continuity: Definition of continuity at a point and in an interval, types.
*   **Chapter 10: Differential Calculus - Differentiability**
    *   Derivative Definition: First principles differentiation, geometric meaning of f'(x).
    *   Differentiation Rules: Sum, product, quotient rules, chain rule differentiation.
    *   Implicit Differentiation: Parametric equations, second-order derivatives calculation.
*   **Chapter 11: Integral Calculus**
    *   Anti-derivative Concept: Indefinite integrals, basic properties, standard forms.
    *   Integration Techniques: Substitution method, integration by parts, partial fractions.
    *   Definite Integrals: Fundamental theorem of calculus, evaluating basic areas.
*   **Chapter 12: Introduction to Probability Theory**
    *   Classical Probability: Sample space, event probability, addition theorem.
    *   Conditional Probability: Multiplication theorem, independent events, total probability.
    *   Bayes Theorem: Formula derivation and simple application word problems.

### Physics Volume 1

*   **Unit 1: Nature of Physical World and Measurement**
    *   Physical Quantities: Fundamental and derived quantities, units systems.
    *   Errors in Measurement: Accuracy, precision, systematic and random errors, propagation.
    *   Dimensional Analysis: Dimensions of quantities, homogeneity principle, limitations.
*   **Unit 2: Kinematics**
    *   Frame of Reference: Position, path length, displacement, speed and velocity.
    *   Equations of Motion: Uniformly accelerated motion, free fall acceleration.
    *   Vector Kinematics: Projectile motion, uniform circular motion equations.
*   **Unit 3: Laws of Motion**
    *   Force and Inertia: Newton's first law, inertia types, momentum.
    *   Newton's Second & Third Laws: F = ma, action-reaction, conservation of momentum, impulse.
    *   Friction and Circular Motion: Static and kinetic friction, banking of roads, centripetal force.
*   **Unit 4: Work, Energy and Power**
    *   Work Done: Work done by constant and variable force, scalar product.
    *   Kinetic & Potential Energy: Work-energy theorem, potential energy of a spring, conservation.
    *   Power and Collisions: Elastic and inelastic collisions, coefficient of restitution.
*   **Unit 5: Motion of System of Particles and Rigid Bodies**
    *   Center of Mass: Definition, calculation for two-particle system, rigid body.
    *   Rotational Kinematics: Torque, angular momentum, conservation of angular momentum.
    *   Moment of Inertia: Radius of gyration, parallel and perpendicular axes theorems.

### Physics Volume 2

*   **Unit 6: Gravitation**
    *   Newton's Law: Universal law of gravitation, Kepler's laws of planetary motion.
    *   Acceleration due to gravity: Variation of g with altitude, depth, and latitude.
    *   Satellites: Orbital velocity, time period, escape speed, weightlessness.
*   **Unit 7: Properties of Matter**
    *   Elasticity: Hooke's law, stress-strain curve, Young's modulus.
    *   Fluid Mechanics: Pascal's law, Archimedes' principle, viscosity, Stokes' law.
    *   Surface Tension: Cohesive/adhesive forces, capillary rise, surface energy.
*   **Unit 8: Heat and Thermodynamics**
    *   Thermal Properties: Temperature scales, thermal expansion, specific heat capacity.
    *   Thermodynamic Laws: Zeroth, first and second laws, state variables.
    *   Heat Engines: Carnot engine efficiency, refrigerator coefficient of performance.
*   **Unit 9: Kinetic Theory of Gases**
    *   Ideal Gas Laws: Molecular nature of matter, gas pressure expression.
    *   Law of Equipartition: Degrees of freedom, specific heats of mono/diatomic gases.
    *   Mean Free Path: Maxwell distribution, mean free path derivation.
*   **Unit 10: Oscillations**
    *   Periodic Motion: Simple harmonic motion (SHM) definition, displacement.
    *   SHM Equations: Velocity, acceleration, energy in SHM, simple pendulum.
    *   Damped & Forced SHM: Resonance, free and forced oscillations properties.
*   **Unit 11: Waves**
    *   Wave Motion: Transverse and longitudinal waves, speed of wave propagation.
    *   Superposition Principle: Standing waves in string, organ pipes, beats phenomenon.
    *   Doppler Effect: Acoustic doppler effect formula derivation, applications.

### Chemistry Volume 1

*   **Chapter 1: Basic Concepts of Chemistry and Calculations**
    *   Classification of Matter: Pure substances, elements, compounds, mole concept.
    *   Stoichiometry: Balancing chemical equations, limiting reagent calculations.
    *   Equivalent Mass: Acid, base and salt equivalent mass calculations.
*   **Chapter 2: Quantum Mechanical Model of Atom**
    *   Sub-atomic Particles: Bohr model, dual nature of matter, Heisenberg uncertainty.
    *   Schrodinger Equation: Quantum numbers (n, l, m, s), shape of orbitals.
    *   Electronic Configurations: Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion principle, Hund's rule.
*   **Chapter 3: Periodic Classification of Elements**
    *   Historical Classification: Dobereiner, Mendeleev periodic law, modern periodic table.
    *   Periodic Properties: Atomic radius, ionization energy, electronegativity variation.
*   **Chapter 4: Hydrogen**
    *   Isotopes of Hydrogen: Orthohydrogen, parahydrogen, heavy water preparation.
    *   Hydrides: Ionic, covalent and metallic hydrides classification.
*   **Chapter 5: Alkali and Alkaline Earth Metals**
    *   Alkali Metals: Group 1 elements properties, anomaly of lithium.
    *   Alkaline Earth Metals: Group 2 elements, quicklime, plaster of paris.
*   **Chapter 6: Gaseous State**
    *   Gas Laws: Boyle's law, Charles's law, Dalton's partial pressure.
    *   Ideal vs Real Gases: Compressibility factor, Van der Waals equation of state.
*   **Chapter 7: Thermodynamics**
    *   Thermodynamic Terms: System, surroundings, extensive and intensive properties.
    *   First & Second Laws: Enthalpy, Hess's law, entropy, Gibbs free energy criteria.

### Chemistry Volume 2

*   **Chapter 8: Physical and Chemical Equilibrium**
    *   Reversible Reactions: Dynamic nature of equilibrium, Kc and Kp relation.
    *   Le Chatelier Principle: Effect of concentration, temperature, pressure changes.
*   **Chapter 9: Solutions**
    *   Solubility Rules: Concentration units (molarity, molality), Henry's law.
    *   Colligative Properties: Raoult's law, elevation of boiling point, osmotic pressure.
*   **Chapter 10: Chemical Bonding**
    *   Bonding Theories: Lewis dot structures, octet rule, VSEPR theory shapes.
    *   Hybridization: Valence bond theory, sp, sp2, sp3 hybrid orbitals model.
*   **Chapter 11: Fundamentals of Organic Chemistry**
    *   Organic Nomenclature: IUPAC naming rules, classification of functional groups.
    *   Isomerism: Structural isomerism, stereoisomerism (geometrical/optical).
*   **Chapter 12: Basic Rules of Organic Reactions**
    *   Electron Displacement: Inductive effect, electromeric, resonance, hyperconjugation.
    *   Reaction Intermediates: Carbocations, carbanions, free radicals stability.
*   **Chapter 13: Hydrocarbons**
    *   Alkanes & Alkenes: Preparation, properties, markownikoff's rule.
    *   Aromatic Hydrocarbons: Benzene structure, Huckel's rule of aromaticity.
*   **Chapter 14: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes**
    *   Nucleophilic Substitution: SN1 and SN2 reaction mechanism comparison.
    *   Haloarenes Properties: Sandmeyer reaction, electrophilic substitution in chlorobenzene.
*   **Chapter 15: Environmental Chemistry**
    *   Pollution Types: Air, water and soil pollution, greenhouse effect, acid rain.
