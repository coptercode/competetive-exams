# Introduction

Plant anatomy deals with the internal structure of plants, while plant physiology deals with the functions and processes occurring in plants.

This chapter covers:

- Plant tissues
- Tissue systems
- Photosynthesis
- Respiration in plants
- Transport in plants

---

# PART A: PLANT TISSUES

---

# 1. Plant Tissue

## Definition

A group of similar cells performing a specific function is called a tissue.

---

Plant tissues are classified into:

1. Meristematic tissue
2. Permanent tissue

---

# PART B: MERISTEMATIC TISSUE

---

# 2. Meristematic Tissue

## Definition

A tissue consisting of actively dividing cells responsible for plant growth is called meristematic tissue.

---

Characteristics:

- Cells divide continuously.
- Cells have dense cytoplasm.
- Large nucleus.
- Small or absent vacuoles.

---

# 3. Types of Meristematic Tissue

---

## 1. Apical Meristem

Location:

- Root tip
- Shoot tip

Function:

\[
\boxed{\text{Increase in length}}
\]

---

## 2. Lateral Meristem

Location:

- Cambium

Function:

\[
\boxed{\text{Increase in girth}}
\]

---

## 3. Intercalary Meristem

Location:

- Base of leaves
- Internodes

Function:

Growth of internodes.

---

# PART C: PERMANENT TISSUE

---

# 4. Permanent Tissue

## Definition

Cells that lose the ability to divide and perform specific functions are called permanent tissues.

---

Types:

1. Simple permanent tissue
2. Complex permanent tissue

---

# 5. Simple Permanent Tissue

---

## Parenchyma

Functions:

- Storage of food
- Photosynthesis

---

## Collenchyma

Functions:

- Provides flexibility
- Supports growing parts

---

## Sclerenchyma

Functions:

- Provides strength and rigidity

---

# 6. Complex Permanent Tissue

Complex tissues contain different types of cells.

Types:

1. Xylem
2. Phloem

---

# 7. Xylem

## Function

Transport of:

\[
\boxed{\text{Water and minerals}}
\]

from roots to other parts.

---

Components:

- Tracheids
- Vessels
- Xylem fibres
- Xylem parenchyma

---

# 8. Phloem

## Function

Transport of:

\[
\boxed{\text{Food materials}}
\]

from leaves to other parts.

---

Components:

- Sieve tubes
- Companion cells
- Phloem fibres
- Phloem parenchyma

---

# PART D: TISSUE SYSTEMS

---

# 9. Plant Tissue Systems

Three tissue systems:

1. Epidermal tissue system
2. Ground tissue system
3. Vascular tissue system

---

# 10. Epidermal Tissue System

Functions:

- Protection
- Prevents water loss

---

Includes:

- Epidermis
- Stomata
- Root hairs

---

# 11. Ground Tissue System

Functions:

- Storage
- Photosynthesis
- Support

---

# 12. Vascular Tissue System

Includes:

- Xylem
- Phloem

Function:

Transport of substances.

---

# PART E: PHOTOSYNTHESIS

---

# 13. Photosynthesis

## Definition

The process by which green plants prepare food using sunlight, carbon dioxide and water is called photosynthesis.

---

Equation:

\[
\boxed{
6CO_2+6H_2O
\rightarrow
C_6H_{12}O_6+6O_2
}
\]

---

Requirements:

- Sunlight
- Chlorophyll
- Carbon dioxide
- Water

---

# 14. Role of Chlorophyll

Chlorophyll absorbs sunlight energy required for photosynthesis.

---

Location:

\[
\boxed{\text{Chloroplast}}
\]

---

# 15. Factors Affecting Photosynthesis

1. Light intensity
2. Carbon dioxide concentration
3. Temperature
4. Water availability

---

# PART F: RESPIRATION IN PLANTS

---

# 16. Respiration

## Definition

The process of breaking down food to release energy is called respiration.

---

Equation:

\[
\boxed{
C_6H_{12}O_6+6O_2
\rightarrow
6CO_2+6H_2O+Energy
}
\]

---

# 17. Types of Respiration

---

## Aerobic Respiration

Occurs in presence of oxygen.

Produces:

- Carbon dioxide
- Water
- Energy

---

## Anaerobic Respiration

Occurs without oxygen.

Example:

Yeast fermentation

---

# PART G: TRANSPORT IN PLANTS

---

# 18. Transport

Plants transport:

- Water
- Minerals
- Food

---

# 19. Water Transport

Water moves through:

\[
\boxed{\text{Xylem}}
\]

---

Process:

Root → Stem → Leaves

---

# 20. Mineral Transport

Minerals are absorbed from soil by roots.

---

Transported through:

\[
\boxed{\text{Xylem}}
\]

---

# 21. Food Transport

Food prepared in leaves is transported through:

\[
\boxed{\text{Phloem}}
\]

---

Process:

\[
\boxed{\text{Translocation}}
\]

---

# 22. Transpiration

## Definition

Loss of water vapour from aerial parts of plants is called transpiration.

---

Occurs mainly through:

\[
\boxed{\text{Stomata}}
\]

---

Functions:

- Cooling of plant
- Helps water movement

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing xylem and phloem functions.
- Forgetting chlorophyll is required for photosynthesis.
- Mixing photosynthesis and respiration equations.
- Confusing meristematic and permanent tissues.
- Forgetting transpiration occurs mainly through stomata.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Plant tissues are groups of specialized cells.
- Meristematic tissues help in growth.
- Xylem transports water and minerals.
- Phloem transports food.
- Photosynthesis produces food using sunlight.
- Respiration releases energy from food.
- Transport systems help plants survive.

\[
