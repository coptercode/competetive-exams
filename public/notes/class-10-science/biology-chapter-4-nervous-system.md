# Introduction

The nervous system controls and coordinates all activities of the body by receiving information and sending responses.

This chapter covers:

- Central nervous system
- Peripheral nervous system
- Neurons
- Reflex action
- Reflex arc

---

# PART A: NERVOUS SYSTEM

---

# 1. Nervous System

## Definition

The nervous system is a system of organs that controls body activities through electrical signals called nerve impulses.

---

Functions:

- Receives information
- Processes information
- Produces responses
- Coordinates body activities

---

# 2. Divisions of Nervous System

The human nervous system is divided into:

1. Central nervous system (CNS)
2. Peripheral nervous system (PNS)

---

# PART B: NEURON

---

# 3. Neuron

## Definition

A neuron is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system.

---

Functions:

- Receives signals
- Transmits nerve impulses

---

# 4. Structure of Neuron

Main parts:

1. Cell body
2. Dendrites
3. Axon

---

# 5. Cell Body

Contains:

- Nucleus
- Cytoplasm

Function:

Controls activities of the neuron.

---

# 6. Dendrites

Function:

\[
\boxed{\text{Receive nerve impulses}}
\]

---

# 7. Axon

Function:

\[
\boxed{\text{Transmits impulses away from cell body}}
\]

---

# PART C: CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM

---

# 8. Central Nervous System (CNS)

## Definition

The nervous system consisting of brain and spinal cord is called the central nervous system.

---

Components:

1. Brain
2. Spinal cord

---

# 9. Brain

## Definition

The brain is the main control centre of the nervous system.

---

Protected by:

- Skull
- Meninges
- Cerebrospinal fluid

---

# 10. Parts of Brain

Main parts:

1. Forebrain
2. Midbrain
3. Hindbrain

---

# 11. Forebrain

Functions:

- Thinking
- Memory
- Intelligence
- Sensory perception

---

Main part:

\[
\boxed{\text{Cerebrum}}
\]

---

# 12. Cerebrum

## Function

Controls:

- Voluntary actions
- Thinking
- Learning
- Reasoning

---

# 13. Midbrain

Functions:

- Controls visual and auditory reflexes.

---

# 14. Hindbrain

Contains:

- Cerebellum
- Pons
- Medulla oblongata

---

## Cerebellum

Function:

- Maintains balance
- Coordinates muscle movements

---

## Medulla Oblongata

Controls involuntary activities:

- Breathing
- Heartbeat
- Digestion

---

# 15. Spinal Cord

## Definition

A long cylindrical structure extending from the brain through the vertebral column.

---

Functions:

- Conducts nerve impulses
- Controls reflex actions

---

# PART D: PERIPHERAL NERVOUS SYSTEM

---

# 16. Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)

## Definition

The nervous system consisting of nerves outside the brain and spinal cord is called peripheral nervous system.

---

Components:

- Cranial nerves
- Spinal nerves

---

# 17. Cranial Nerves

Nerves arising from the brain are called cranial nerves.

---

Number:

\[
\boxed{12\ pairs}
\]

---

# 18. Spinal Nerves

Nerves arising from spinal cord.

---

Number:

\[
\boxed{31\ pairs}
\]

---

# PART E: TYPES OF NERVES

---

# 19. Sensory Nerves

Function:

Carry impulses from sense organs to brain/spinal cord.

---

# 20. Motor Nerves

Function:

Carry impulses from brain/spinal cord to muscles and glands.

---

# 21. Mixed Nerves

Contain both:

- Sensory fibres
- Motor fibres

---

# PART F: REFLEX ACTION

---

# 22. Reflex Action

## Definition

A quick automatic response to a stimulus without conscious thinking is called reflex action.

---

Examples:

- Removing hand from hot object
- Blinking of eyes

---

# 23. Reflex Arc

## Definition

The pathway followed by nerve impulses during a reflex action is called reflex arc.

---

Path:

Stimulus

↓

Receptor

↓

Sensory neuron

↓

Spinal cord

↓

Motor neuron

↓

Effector

↓

Response

---

# 24. Components of Reflex Arc

1. Receptor
2. Sensory neuron
3. Interneuron
4. Motor neuron
5. Effector organ

---

# PART G: VOLUNTARY AND INVOLUNTARY ACTIONS

---

# 25. Voluntary Actions

Actions under our conscious control.

Examples:

- Walking
- Writing

Controlled by:

\[
\boxed{\text{Cerebrum}}
\]

---

# 26. Involuntary Actions

Actions that occur automatically.

Examples:

- Heartbeat
- Breathing

Controlled by:

\[
\boxed{\text{Medulla oblongata}}
\]

---

# Important Points

- Neuron is the basic unit of nervous system.
- Brain and spinal cord form CNS.
- Nerves form PNS.
- Cerebrum controls thinking and voluntary actions.
- Cerebellum maintains balance.
- Medulla controls involuntary actions.
- Reflex actions provide quick protection.

---

---

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing cerebrum and cerebellum.
- Forgetting spinal cord is part of CNS.
- Mixing sensory and motor nerves.
- Confusing voluntary and involuntary actions.
- Forgetting reflex action is mainly controlled by spinal cord.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Nervous system controls and coordinates body activities.
- Neurons transmit nerve impulses.
- CNS consists of brain and spinal cord.
- PNS consists of nerves outside CNS.
- Reflex actions provide immediate responses to harmful stimuli.

\[
\boxed{
\text{The nervous system acts as the communication network of the body.}
}
