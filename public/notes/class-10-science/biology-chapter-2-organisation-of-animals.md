# Introduction

Animals have different levels of organisation that help them perform various life processes efficiently.

This chapter covers:

- Animal organisation
- Leech morphology
- Rabbit morphology
- Organ systems and functions

---

# PART A: LEVELS OF ORGANISATION IN ANIMALS

---

# 1. Levels of Organisation

Animals show different levels of organisation:

1. Cellular level
2. Tissue level
3. Organ level
4. Organ system level

---

# 2. Cellular Level

## Definition

In some animals, cells perform all life activities independently.

Example:

\[
\boxed{\text{Protozoans}}
\]

---

# 3. Tissue Level

Groups of similar cells form tissues.

Example:

\[
\boxed{\text{Hydra}}
\]

---

# 4. Organ Level

Different tissues combine to form organs.

Example:

\[
\boxed{\text{Flatworms}}
\]

---

# 5. Organ System Level

Different organs work together as organ systems.

Examples:

- Human beings
- Rabbit

---

# PART B: LEVEES IN ANIMALS

---

# 6. Leech

## Scientific Name

\[
\boxed{\textit{Hirudinaria granulosa}}
\]

---

# 7. Habitat of Leech

Leeches are commonly found in:

- Freshwater ponds
- Marshy areas

---

# 8. External Features of Leech

---

## Body Shape

- Elongated
- Cylindrical body
- Segmented

---

## Suckers

Two suckers are present:

1. Anterior sucker
2. Posterior sucker

---

Functions:

- Attachment
- Movement
- Feeding

---

## Segmentation

The body is divided into many segments called annuli.

---

# 9. Nutrition in Leech

Leech is a blood-sucking animal.

---

Special structure:

\[
\boxed{\text{Sucker}}
\]

---

It secretes:

\[
\boxed{\text{Hirudin}}
\]

---

Function of hirudin:

Prevents blood clotting.

---

# 10. Respiration in Leech

Respiration occurs through:

\[
\boxed{\text{Skin}}
\]

---

# 11. Excretion in Leech

Excretion occurs through:

\[
\boxed{\text{Nephridia}}
\]

---

# PART C: RABBIT

---

# 12. Rabbit

## Scientific Name

\[
\boxed{\textit{Oryctolagus cuniculus}}
\]

---

# 13. Habitat

Rabbit is a terrestrial mammal.

---

# 14. External Features of Rabbit

---

## Body Division

Body is divided into:

1. Head
2. Neck
3. Trunk
4. Tail

---

# 15. Head

Features:

- Eyes
- Ears
- Nose
- Mouth

---

# 16. Eyes

Functions:

- Vision
- Protection

---

# 17. Ears

Features:

- Large external ears
- Helps in hearing

---

# 18. Teeth

Rabbit has:

- Incisors
- Premolars
- Molars

---

Special feature:

\[
\boxed{\text{Incisors keep growing continuously}}
\]

---

# 19. Limbs

Two pairs of limbs:

---

## Forelimbs

Used for:

- Digging
- Holding food

---

## Hind limbs

Used for:

- Jumping
- Running

---

# PART D: INTERNAL ORGANISATION OF RABBIT

---

# 20. Digestive System

Parts:

1. Mouth
2. Oesophagus
3. Stomach
4. Small intestine
5. Large intestine
6. Anus

---

Function:

Digestion and absorption of food.

---

# 21. Respiratory System

Parts:

- Nostrils
- Trachea
- Lungs

---

Function:

Exchange of gases.

---

# 22. Circulatory System

Rabbit has:

\[
\boxed{\text{Closed circulatory system}}
\]

---

Heart has:

\[
\boxed{4\ chambers}
\]

---

Function:

Transport of oxygen and nutrients.

---

# 23. Nervous System

Parts:

- Brain
- Spinal cord
- Nerves

---

Function:

Coordination and control.

---

# 24. Reproductive System

Rabbit reproduces sexually.

---

Female rabbit:

- Produces eggs
- Gives birth to young ones

---

# PART E: COMPARISON BETWEEN LEECH AND RABBIT

| Leech                   | Rabbit                 |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Invertebrate            | Vertebrate             |
| No backbone             | Backbone present       |
| Breathes through skin   | Breathes through lungs |
| Blood-sucking animal    | Herbivorous mammal     |
| Nephridia for excretion | Kidneys for excretion  |

---

# Important Points

- Leech belongs to phylum Annelida.
- Rabbit belongs to class Mammalia.
- Leech uses suckers for attachment.
- Hirudin prevents blood clotting.
- Rabbit has a four-chambered heart.
- Mammals have hair and mammary glands.

---

---

\[
\boxed{\text{Prevents blood clotting}}
\]

---

## Example 2

Which organ helps in respiration of rabbit?

Answer:

\[
\boxed{\text{Lungs}}
\]

---

\[

\]

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing vertebrates and invertebrates.
- Forgetting leech respiration occurs through skin.
- Confusing nephridia with kidneys.
- Forgetting rabbit is a mammal.
- Mixing external and internal features.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Animals show different levels of organisation.
- Leech is an invertebrate with suckers and segmented body.
- Rabbit is a vertebrate mammal with complex organ systems.
- Different organs work together for survival.
