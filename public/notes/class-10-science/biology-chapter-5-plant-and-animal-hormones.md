# Introduction

Hormones are chemical messengers produced by living organisms that regulate growth, development and various body functions.

This chapter covers:

- Plant hormones
- Auxins
- Gibberellins
- Animal hormones
- Thyroid hormone
- Insulin
- Adrenaline

---

# PART A: HORMONES

---

# 1. Hormone

## Definition

A hormone is a chemical substance produced in small quantities by endocrine glands or plant tissues that controls specific activities in the body.

---

Functions:

- Growth regulation
- Metabolism control
- Reproduction
- Maintaining body balance

---

# PART B: PLANT HORMONES

---

# 2. Plant Hormones

## Definition

Chemical substances produced by plants that regulate growth and development are called plant hormones or phytohormones.

---

Major plant hormones:

1. Auxins
2. Gibberellins
3. Cytokinins
4. Abscisic acid
5. Ethylene

---

# 3. Auxins

## Definition

Auxins are plant hormones that promote cell growth and elongation.

---

Produced mainly in:

\[
\boxed{\text{Shoot tips}}
\]

---

Functions:

- Promotes cell elongation
- Helps root formation
- Controls phototropism
- Prevents premature leaf fall

---

# 4. Phototropism

## Definition

The growth movement of plants in response to light is called phototropism.

---

Example:

A plant shoot bends towards sunlight.

---

Role of auxin:

Auxin accumulates on the shaded side and causes faster growth.

---

# 5. Gibberellins

## Definition

Gibberellins are plant hormones that promote growth and development.

---

Functions:

- Increase stem length
- Promote seed germination
- Increase fruit size
- Help flowering

---

# 6. Cytokinins

## Function:

- Promote cell division
- Delay ageing of leaves

---

# 7. Abscisic Acid (ABA)

## Definition

ABA is a plant hormone that inhibits growth.

---

Functions:

- Causes dormancy
- Causes stomatal closure
- Prevents water loss

---

Also called:

\[
\boxed{\text{Stress hormone}}
\]

---

# 8. Ethylene

## Definition

Ethylene is a gaseous plant hormone involved in fruit ripening.

---

Functions:

- Fruit ripening
- Leaf fall
- Flower ageing

---

# PART C: ANIMAL HORMONES

---

# 9. Endocrine Glands

## Definition

Glands that release hormones directly into blood are called endocrine glands.

---

Examples:

- Thyroid gland
- Pancreas
- Adrenal gland

---

# PART D: THYROID HORMONE

---

# 10. Thyroid Gland

Location:

\[
\boxed{\text{Neck region}}
\]

---

Hormone produced:

\[
\boxed{\text{Thyroxine}}
\]

---

Requires:

\[
\boxed{\text{Iodine}}
\]

---

# 11. Functions of Thyroxine

- Controls metabolism
- Regulates growth
- Maintains body temperature

---

# 12. Thyroid Disorders

---

## Goitre

Enlargement of thyroid gland due to iodine deficiency.

---

Prevention:

Adequate iodine intake.

---

# PART E: INSULIN

---

# 13. Insulin

## Definition

Insulin is a hormone produced by pancreas that regulates blood glucose level.

---

Produced by:

\[
\boxed{\text{Beta cells of pancreas}}
\]

---

# 14. Functions of Insulin

- Decreases blood sugar level
- Helps glucose absorption by cells
- Converts excess glucose into glycogen

---

# 15. Diabetes Mellitus

## Definition

A disorder caused by insufficient insulin production or improper insulin action is called diabetes mellitus.

---

Symptoms:

- Increased thirst
- Frequent urination
- Increased hunger
- Fatigue

---

# PART F: ADRENALINE

---

# 16. Adrenal Gland

Location:

Above kidneys.

---

Hormone produced:

\[
\boxed{\text{Adrenaline}}
\]

---

# 17. Adrenaline

## Definition

Adrenaline is a hormone released during emergency situations.

---

Also called:

\[
\boxed{\text{Emergency hormone}}
\]

---

# 18. Functions of Adrenaline

During stress or danger:

- Increases heartbeat
- Increases breathing rate
- Increases blood pressure
- Provides quick energy

---

# PART G: COMPARISON

| Plant Hormones        | Animal Hormones              |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Produced by plants    | Produced by endocrine glands |
| Regulate plant growth | Control body functions       |
| Example: Auxin        | Example: Insulin             |

---

# Important Points

- Auxins promote plant growth.
- Gibberellins increase stem growth.
- ABA inhibits growth.
- Ethylene helps fruit ripening.
- Thyroxine controls metabolism.
- Insulin controls blood glucose.
- Adrenaline prepares body for emergencies.

---

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing auxin and gibberellin functions.
- Forgetting iodine is required for thyroid hormone production.
- Mixing insulin and adrenaline functions.
- Forgetting hormones act as chemical messengers.
- Confusing endocrine glands with exocrine glands.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Hormones regulate growth and body functions.
- Plant hormones control growth and development.
- Auxins and gibberellins promote growth.
- Thyroxine regulates metabolism.
- Insulin controls blood glucose.
- Adrenaline prepares the body for emergency responses.
