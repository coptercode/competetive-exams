# Introduction

Health is the condition of physical, mental and social well-being of an individual.

Diseases affect the normal functioning of the body and may occur due to infections, lifestyle changes or genetic factors.

This chapter covers:

- Health and hygiene
- Drug abuse
- Diabetes
- Obesity
- Heart diseases
- AIDS
- Prevention and control

---

# PART A: HEALTH AND DISEASE

---

# 1. Health

## Definition

Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease.

---

Factors affecting health:

- Nutrition
- Clean environment
- Exercise
- Personal hygiene
- Mental well-being

---

# 2. Disease

## Definition

A disease is a condition that affects the normal functioning of the body.

---

Types:

1. Infectious diseases
2. Non-infectious diseases

---

# PART B: INFECTIOUS DISEASES

---

# 3. Infectious Diseases

## Definition

Diseases caused by pathogens and transmitted from one person to another are called infectious diseases.

---

Caused by:

- Bacteria
- Viruses
- Fungi
- Protozoans

---

Examples:

- Tuberculosis
- Malaria
- AIDS

---

# 4. Prevention of Infectious Diseases

Methods:

- Maintain hygiene
- Drink clean water
- Vaccination
- Proper sanitation
- Avoid contact with infected persons

---

# PART C: DRUG ABUSE

---

# 5. Drug Abuse

## Definition

The misuse of drugs that affects physical and mental health is called drug abuse.

---

Causes:

- Peer pressure
- Stress
- Curiosity
- Lack of awareness

---

# 6. Effects of Drug Abuse

Physical effects:

- Damage to organs
- Weak immunity
- Poor health

Mental effects:

- Depression
- Anxiety
- Loss of concentration

Social effects:

- Family problems
- Poor academic performance
- Crime

---

# 7. Prevention of Drug Abuse

- Awareness programmes
- Counselling
- Healthy lifestyle
- Avoid bad company

---

# PART D: DIABETES

---

# 8. Diabetes Mellitus

## Definition

Diabetes is a disorder in which blood glucose level becomes abnormally high due to lack of insulin or improper insulin function.

---

Hormone involved:

\[
\boxed{\text{Insulin}}
\]

---

# 9. Types of Diabetes

---

## Type 1 Diabetes

- Insulin production is very low.
- Usually develops at younger age.

---

## Type 2 Diabetes

- Body cells do not respond properly to insulin.
- Common in adults.

---

# 10. Symptoms of Diabetes

- Increased thirst
- Frequent urination
- Increased hunger
- Weight loss
- Tiredness

---

# 11. Prevention and Control

- Balanced diet
- Regular exercise
- Maintain healthy weight
- Regular medical check-up

---

# PART E: OBESITY

---

# 12. Obesity

## Definition

Excess accumulation of body fat leading to increased body weight is called obesity.

---

Causes:

- High calorie diet
- Lack of exercise
- Genetic factors
- Sedentary lifestyle

---

# 13. Effects of Obesity

Can cause:

- Diabetes
- Heart diseases
- High blood pressure
- Joint problems

---

# 14. Prevention of Obesity

- Healthy diet
- Physical activity
- Avoid junk food
- Maintain proper body weight

---

# PART F: HEART DISEASES

---

# 15. Cardiovascular Diseases

## Definition

Diseases affecting the heart and blood vessels are called cardiovascular diseases.

---

# 16. Common Heart Diseases

---

## Coronary Artery Disease

Caused by blockage of coronary arteries.

---

## Hypertension

High blood pressure condition.

---

## Heart Attack

Occurs when blood supply to heart muscle is blocked.

---

# 17. Risk Factors

- Smoking
- Obesity
- High cholesterol
- Lack of exercise
- Stress
- Unhealthy diet

---

# 18. Prevention

- Regular exercise
- Balanced diet
- Avoid smoking
- Control blood pressure
- Reduce stress

---

# PART G: AIDS

---

# 19. AIDS

## Full Form:

\[
\boxed{\text{Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome}}
\]

---

Caused by:

\[
\boxed{\text{HIV virus}}
\]

---

# 20. HIV

## Full Form:

\[
\boxed{\text{Human Immunodeficiency Virus}}
\]

---

HIV attacks:

\[
\boxed{\text{Immune system}}
\]

especially:

\[
\boxed{\text{T-helper cells}}
\]

---

# 21. Transmission of HIV

HIV spreads through:

- Infected blood
- Unprotected sexual contact
- Sharing contaminated needles
- Infected mother to child

---

# 22. HIV Does Not Spread Through

- Handshake
- Sharing food
- Hugging
- Normal social contact

---

# 23. Prevention of AIDS

- Safe blood transfusion
- Avoid sharing needles
- Safe sexual practices
- Awareness programmes

---

# PART H: IMMUNITY

---

# 24. Immunity

## Definition

The ability of the body to resist diseases is called immunity.

---

Types:

1. Innate immunity
2. Acquired immunity

---

# 25. Vaccination

## Definition

The process of introducing weakened or inactive pathogens to develop immunity is called vaccination.

---

Function:

- Stimulates immune system
- Prevents diseases

---

# Important Points

- Good health requires physical and mental well-being.
- Drug abuse affects individuals and society.
- Insulin controls blood glucose level.
- Obesity increases risk of many diseases.
- Heart diseases are linked with lifestyle factors.
- AIDS is caused by HIV and affects immunity.

---

---

\[

\]

---

## Example 2

What causes AIDS?

Answer:

\[
\boxed{\text{HIV virus}}
\]

---

## Example 3

Answer:

\[
\boxed{\text{Vaccination}}
\]

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing HIV and AIDS.
- Thinking AIDS spreads through casual contact.
- Forgetting insulin controls glucose level.
- Mixing infectious and non-infectious diseases.
- Ignoring lifestyle factors in heart diseases.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Health depends on physical, mental and social well-being.
- Diseases can be infectious or non-infectious.
- Drug abuse damages health and society.
- Diabetes occurs due to insulin problems.
- Obesity increases disease risk.
- AIDS is caused by HIV and weakens immunity.
- Prevention and healthy habits help maintain good health.
