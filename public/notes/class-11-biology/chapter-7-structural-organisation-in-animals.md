# Introduction

Animals have complex body organisations where cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs form organ systems. This chapter explains the structural organisation and physiological systems of the frog.

This chapter covers:

- Animal tissues
- Frog morphology
- Digestive system
- Circulatory system
- Respiratory system
- Nervous system
- Reproductive system

---

# PART A: ANIMAL TISSUES

---

# 1. Animal Tissue

## Definition

A group of similar cells performing a specific function in animals is called animal tissue.

---

# Types of Animal Tissues

1. Epithelial tissue
2. Connective tissue
3. Muscular tissue
4. Nervous tissue

---

# 2. Epithelial Tissue

## Definition

The tissue that covers body surfaces and lines internal organs is called epithelial tissue.

---

Functions:

- Protection
- Absorption
- Secretion
- Excretion

---

# Types of Epithelial Tissue

---

## A. Simple Epithelium

Made up of a single layer of cells.

---

Types:

### Squamous Epithelium

Function:

- Diffusion

Example:

Blood vessels

---

### Cuboidal Epithelium

Function:

- Absorption and secretion

Example:

Kidney tubules

---

### Columnar Epithelium

Function:

- Absorption

Example:

Intestine

---

### Ciliated Epithelium

Function:

- Movement of substances

Example:

Respiratory tract

---

# B. Compound Epithelium

Multiple layers of cells.

Function:

- Protection

Example:

Skin

---

# 3. Connective Tissue

## Definition

A tissue that connects, supports and binds body parts is called connective tissue.

---

Examples:

- Blood
- Bone
- Cartilage
- Tendon
- Ligament

---

# 4. Muscular Tissue

## Function

Responsible for body movement.

---

Types:

1. Striated muscle
2. Smooth muscle
3. Cardiac muscle

---

# 5. Nervous Tissue

## Function

Receives and transmits nerve impulses.

---

Basic unit:

\[
\boxed{\text{Neuron}}
\]

# Important Differences

## Arteries vs Veins

| Arteries                    | Veins                |
| --------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Carry blood away from heart | Carry blood to heart |
| Thick walls                 | Thin walls           |
| High pressure               | Low pressure         |

---

## Frog vs Human Heart

| Frog                  | Human               |
| --------------------- | ------------------- |
| Three chambers        | Four chambers       |
| Incomplete separation | Complete separation |
| Single circulation    | Double circulation  |

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing frog and human heart structure.
- Forgetting frogs have three types of respiration.
- Mixing arteries and veins.
- Forgetting fertilisation in frogs is external.
- Confusing CNS and PNS.

# Chapter Summary

- Animal body organisation involves tissues, organs and systems.
- Frog belongs to class Amphibia.
- Frog has specialised digestive, circulatory and respiratory systems.
- Frog heart has three chambers.
- Frogs breathe through skin, buccal cavity and lungs.
- Nervous and reproductive systems coordinate body functions.
