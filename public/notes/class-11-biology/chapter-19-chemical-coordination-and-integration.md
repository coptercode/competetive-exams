# Introduction

Chemical coordination in animals is controlled by hormones secreted by endocrine glands. Hormones act as chemical messengers and regulate growth, metabolism, reproduction and other body functions.

This chapter covers:

- Endocrine glands
- Hormones
- Human endocrine system
- Hypothalamus
- Pituitary gland
- Thyroid gland
- Adrenal gland
- Pancreas
- Gonads
- Mechanism of hormone action
- Hormonal disorders

---

# PART A: ENDOCRINE SYSTEM

---

# 1. Endocrine System

## Definition

The system consisting of ductless glands that release hormones directly into blood is called the endocrine system.

---

# 2. Hormones

## Definition

Hormones are chemical messengers secreted by endocrine glands that regulate body functions.

---

Characteristics:

- Produced in small quantities.
- Transported through blood.
- Act on specific target organs.
- Control growth, metabolism and reproduction.

---

# 3. Endocrine Glands

Major endocrine glands:

1. Hypothalamus
2. Pituitary gland
3. Pineal gland
4. Thyroid gland
5. Parathyroid gland
6. Adrenal gland
7. Pancreas
8. Gonads

---

# PART B: HYPOTHALAMUS

---

# 4. Hypothalamus

## Location

Located in the brain below the thalamus.

---

Functions:

- Controls pituitary gland.
- Maintains body temperature.
- Regulates hunger and thirst.

---

# 5. Hypothalamic Hormones

Two types:

1. Releasing hormones
2. Inhibiting hormones

---

Examples:

- Growth hormone releasing hormone (GHRH)
- Thyrotropin releasing hormone (TRH)

---

---

---

\boxed{\text{Master gland}}

1. Anterior pituitary

# Thyroid Disorders

---

## Hypothyroidism

Low thyroid hormone production.

Effects:

- Slow metabolism.
- Weight gain.

---

## Goitre

Enlargement of thyroid gland.

Cause:

\[
\boxed{\text{Iodine deficiency}}
\]

---

## Cretinism

Hypothyroidism in children.

Effects:

- Poor physical and mental development.

---

## Exophthalmic Goitre

Due to excess thyroid hormones.

Symptoms:

- Enlarged eyes.
- Increased metabolism.

---

# PART J: MECHANISM OF HORMONE ACTION

---

# 20. Hormone Action

Hormones act by binding to specific receptors on target cells.

---

Types:

1. Intracellular receptor mechanism
2. Membrane receptor mechanism

---

# 21. Steroid Hormones

Examples:

- Cortisol
- Testosterone

---

Mechanism:

- Enter cell.
- Bind receptor.
- Affect gene expression.

---

# 22. Protein Hormones

Examples:

- Insulin
- Growth hormone

---

Mechanism:

- Bind membrane receptors.
- Activate second messenger systems.

# PART L: ENDOCRINE DISORDERS

---

# 24. Dwarfism

Cause:

Deficiency of growth hormone during childhood.

---

Effect:

- Short stature.

---

# 25. Acromegaly

Cause:

Excess growth hormone in adults.

---

Effects:

- Enlargement of hands, feet and facial bones.

---

# 26. Cretinism

Cause:

Low thyroid hormone in children.

---

Effects:

- Poor growth.
- Mental development problems.

---

# 27. Goitre

Cause:

Iodine deficiency or thyroid imbalance.

---

Effect:

- Enlarged thyroid gland.

---

# 28. Diabetes Mellitus

Cause:

Insulin deficiency or resistance.

---

Effect:

- Increased blood glucose.

---

# 29. Addison's Disease

Cause:

Insufficient adrenal cortex hormones.

---

Symptoms:

- Weakness.
- Low blood pressure.
- Fatigue.

# Important Differences

## Endocrine vs Exocrine Glands

| Endocrine Glands            | Exocrine Glands                  |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Ductless                    | Have ducts                       |
| Release hormones into blood | Release secretions through ducts |
| Example: Pituitary          | Example: Salivary gland          |

---

## Insulin vs Glucagon

| Insulin                 | Glucagon                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Decreases blood glucose | Increases blood glucose |
| Secreted by beta cells  | Secreted by alpha cells |

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing endocrine and exocrine glands.
- Mixing insulin and glucagon functions.
- Forgetting iodine is required for thyroid hormone synthesis.
- Confusing dwarfism and gigantism.
- Mixing adrenal cortex and adrenal medulla hormones.

- Endocrine glands release hormones into blood.
- Pituitary gland controls many endocrine functions.
- Thyroid regulates metabolism.
- Pancreas controls blood glucose.

- Hormonal imbalance causes endocrine disorders.
