# Introduction

The **molecular basis of inheritance** explains how genetic information is:

- Stored
- Copied
- Expressed
- Passed from one generation to another

The hereditary material in most organisms is:

```
DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid)
```

---

# 1. Genetic Material

## Definition

A genetic material is a molecule that carries hereditary information from parents to offspring.

---

# Characteristics of Genetic Material

A genetic material should:

1. Replicate itself.
2. Remain stable.
3. Store information.
4. Express information.
5. Undergo variation.

---

# 2. DNA as Genetic Material

Scientists proved DNA is the genetic material through experiments.

---

# Griffith's Transformation Experiment (1928)

## Scientist:

Frederick Griffith

## Organism:

```
Streptococcus pneumoniae
```

---

# Bacterial Types

## S-Strain

- Smooth colonies
- Virulent
- Causes pneumonia

---

## R-Strain

- Rough colonies
- Non-virulent

---

# Experiment

```
Live S bacteria
        ↓
Disease
        ↓

Heat killed S bacteria
        +
Live R bacteria
        ↓
Transformation
        ↓
Live S bacteria formed
```

---

# Conclusion

A transforming principle converted R bacteria into S bacteria.

---

# 3. Avery, MacLeod and McCarty Experiment


# 4. Hershey-Chase Experiment

## Organism:

Bacteriophage T2

---

# Experiment

Used radioactive labels:

DNA:

```
32P
```

Protein:

```
35S
```

---

# Result

Only DNA entered bacteria.

---

# Conclusion

DNA is the genetic material.

# 13. Lac Operon

## Discovery:

Jacob and Monod

---

# Definition

A gene regulation system in bacteria controlling lactose metabolism.

---

# Components

```
Lac Operon

Regulator Gene
       │
Promoter
       │
Operator
       │
Structural Genes
```

---

# Structural Genes

Three genes:

## z gene

Produces β-galactosidase.

---

## y gene

Produces permease.

---

## a gene

Produces transacetylase.

---

# In Absence of Lactose

Repressor binds operator.

Genes remain OFF.

---

# In Presence of Lactose

Lactose inactivates repressor.

Genes become ON.

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. Why is DNA considered genetic material?

**Answer:**

DNA can replicate, store genetic information, express traits, and undergo mutations.

---

## 2. Explain semi-conservative replication.

**Answer:**

Each daughter DNA molecule contains one parental strand and one newly synthesized strand.

---

## 3. What is central dogma?

**Answer:**

The flow of genetic information:

```
DNA → RNA → Protein
```

---

## 4. Explain lac operon.

**Answer:**

Lac operon regulates lactose metabolism in bacteria by switching genes ON or OFF depending on lactose availability.

---
