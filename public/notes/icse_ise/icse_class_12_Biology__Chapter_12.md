## Definition

Plants modified by introducing desired genes to improve characteristics.

---

# Objectives

GM crops are developed for:

- Pest resistance

- Higher yield
- Improved nutrition
- Stress tolerance

---

# 4. Bt Cotton

## Definition

A genetically modified cotton variety resistant to insect pests.

---

# Source Gene

Obtained from:

```
Bacillus thuringiensis
```

---

# Bt Toxin

Bt bacteria produce:

```
Cry protein
```

---

# Mechanism

```
Bt Gene Introduced
        ↓
Cotton Plant Produces Cry Protein
        ↓
Insect Feeds on Plant
        ↓
Toxin Activated in Insect Gut
        ↓
Insect Dies
```

---

# Advantages

- Reduces pesticide usage.
- Increases crop yield.
- Provides pest resistance.

---

# 10. Gene Therapy

## Definition

A technique in which defective genes are replaced or corrected to treat genetic disorders.

---

# First Gene Therapy Trial

Disease:

```

```

---

# Adenosine Deaminase (ADA) Deficiency

Cause:

Effect:

---

# Gene Therapy Process

```
Normal Gene Introduced

        ↓

Patient Cells Modified

        ↓

Functional Protein Produced

        ↓

Disease Condition Improved
```

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. What is Bt cotton?

**Answer:**

Bt cotton is a genetically modified cotton variety containing Bt toxin genes from Bacillus thuringiensis, providing resistance against insect pests.

---

## 2. Explain gene therapy.

**Answer:**

Gene therapy involves introducing functional genes into cells to correct defective genes responsible for genetic disorders.

---

## 3. What is Golden Rice?

**Answer:**

Golden Rice is genetically modified rice enriched with beta-carotene, a precursor of vitamin A.

---

## 4. What is ELISA?

**Answer:**

ELISA is a diagnostic technique based on antigen-antibody interaction.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing Bt bacteria and Bt toxin.
- Mixing gene therapy with genetic engineering.
- Forgetting PCR applications.
- Confusing GMO and transgenic organisms.
- Writing insulin as extracted directly from humans.
