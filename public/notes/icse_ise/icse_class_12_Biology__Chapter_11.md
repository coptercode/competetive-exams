# Introduction

**Biotechnology** is the application of biological organisms, cells, enzymes, and molecular techniques to develop useful products and processes.

It combines:

- Biology
- Genetics
- Microbiology
- Molecular biology
- Engineering

---

# Applications of Biotechnology

Biotechnology is used in:

- Medicine
- Agriculture
- Industry
- Environmental management
- Genetic research

# 5. Restriction Enzymes

## Definition

Enzymes that cut DNA at specific recognition sequences.

Also called:

**Molecular scissors**

---

# Discovered by:

Werner Arber, Hamilton Smith, Daniel Nathans

---

# Recognition Sequence

Specific DNA sequence where enzyme cuts.

Example:

```
Palindrome sequence
```

---

# Example

EcoRI

Source:

```
Escherichia coli
```

---

# Function

Cuts DNA producing:

- Sticky ends
- Blunt ends

---

# Sticky Ends

DNA ends with overhanging sequences.

Advantages:

- Easy joining of DNA fragments.

---

# 13. Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)

## Definition

PCR is a technique used to amplify a specific DNA segment.

---

Developed by:

## Kary Mullis

---

# Requirements

- Template DNA
- Primers
- Taq polymerase
- Nucleotides

---

# Steps of PCR

```
PCR

 │

 ├── Denaturation

 │

 ├── Annealing

 │

 └── Extension
```

---

# 1. Denaturation

DNA double strand separates.

Temperature:

Approximately:

```
94°C
```

---

# 2. Annealing

Primers attach to DNA template.

Temperature:

Approximately:

```
50-65°C
```

---

# 3. Extension

DNA synthesis occurs.

Enzyme:

```
Taq polymerase
```

Temperature:

Approximately:

```
72°C
```

---

# Applications of PCR

- Disease diagnosis
- DNA fingerprinting
- Gene cloning
- Research

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. What are restriction enzymes?

**Answer:**

Restriction enzymes are enzymes that cut DNA at specific recognition sequences.

---

## 2. Explain PCR.

**Answer:**

PCR is a technique used to amplify specific DNA sequences through denaturation, annealing, and extension steps.

---

## 3. What is a cloning vector?

**Answer:**

A cloning vector is a DNA molecule used to transfer foreign DNA into a host cell.

---

## 4. What is the role of DNA ligase?

**Answer:**

DNA ligase joins DNA fragments by forming phosphodiester bonds.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing restriction enzymes and ligase.
- Writing PCR steps in wrong order.
- Forgetting Taq polymerase source.
- Mixing vectors and host cells.
- Confusing transformation and transduction.

# Chapter Summary

- Bioreactors enable large-scale production of biotechnology products.
- Biotechnology plays a major role in medicine, agriculture, industry, and research.
