# Introduction

**Microbes** are microscopic organisms that include:

- Bacteria
- Fungi
- Protozoans
- Algae
- Viruses

Although some microbes cause diseases, many are beneficial and are used in:

- Food production
- Medicine
- Agriculture
- Environmental management
- Industrial processes

---

# 1. Microbes in Household Products

Microorganisms are used traditionally for preparing fermented foods.

---

# 1. Curd Formation

Microorganism:

```
Lactobacillus
```

---

# Process

Milk
↓  
Lactobacillus multiplication  
↓  
Conversion of lactose into lactic acid  
↓  
Curd formation

# Importance of Curd

- Improves nutritional value.
- Increases vitamin B₁₂ content.
- Improves digestion.

# 8. Microbes in Sewage Treatment

Wastewater treatment uses microbes to remove organic pollutants.

---

# Sewage Treatment Steps

```
Sewage

  ↓

Primary Treatment

  ↓

Secondary Treatment

  ↓

Activated Sludge

  ↓

Clean Water
```

---

# 9. Primary Treatment

## Definition

Physical removal of large particles.

Includes:

- Filtration
- Sedimentation

---

# 10. Secondary Treatment

## Definition

Biological treatment using microbes.

---

Microorganisms form:

```
Flocs
```

---

# Flocs

Masses of:

- Bacteria
- Fungal filaments

---

# Function

They consume organic matter.

---

# Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD)

## Definition

Amount of oxygen required by microorganisms to decompose organic matter.

---

# Importance

Higher BOD:

→ More pollution

Lower BOD:

→ Cleaner water

---

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. Name the organism used in curd formation.

**Answer:**

Lactobacillus

---

## 2. What is BOD?

**Answer:**

BOD is the amount of oxygen required by microorganisms to decompose organic matter in water.

---

## 3. Explain biogas production.

**Answer:**

Biogas is produced by anaerobic digestion of organic matter by methanogenic bacteria.

---

## 4. What are biofertilizers?

**Answer:**

Biofertilizers are organisms that increase soil fertility by improving nutrient availability.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing fermentation with respiration.
- Mixing antibiotics and enzymes.
- Forgetting methane-producing organisms.
- Confusing biofertilizers with chemical fertilizers.
- Writing BOD as oxygen present instead of oxygen required.
