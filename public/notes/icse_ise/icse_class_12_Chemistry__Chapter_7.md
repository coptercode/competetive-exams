# 1. Alcohols

## Definition

Alcohols are organic compounds containing a hydroxyl group (-OH) attached to an sp³ hybridised carbon atom.

General formula:

\[
R-OH
\]

---

# Examples

## Methanol

\[
CH_3OH
\]

---

## Ethanol

\[
C_2H_5OH
\]

---

# 2. Classification of Alcohols

Based on the carbon attached to -OH group.

---

# Primary Alcohol (1°)

OH group attached to primary carbon.

Example:

\[
CH_3CH_2OH
\]

---

# Secondary Alcohol (2°)

OH group attached to secondary carbon.

Example:

\[
CH_3CHOHCH_3
\]

---

# Tertiary Alcohol (3°)

OH group attached to tertiary carbon.

Example:

\[
(CH_3)_3COH
\]

---

# 3. Nomenclature of Alcohols

Rules:

1. Select longest carbon chain containing -OH.
2. Replace ending "e" with "ol".
3. Give lowest number to -OH group.

---

Examples:

\[
CH_3OH
\]

Name:

```
Methanol
```

---

\[
CH_3CH_2OH
\]

Name:

```
Ethanol
```

---

\[
CH_3CHOHCH_3
\]

Name:

```
Propan-2-ol
```

---

# 4. Preparation of Alcohols

---

# 1. From Alkenes

Addition of water.

Example:

\[
CH_2=CH_2+H_2O
\rightarrow
C_2H_5OH
\]

---

# 2. Hydrolysis of Haloalkanes

Reaction:

\[
RX+KOH(aq)
\rightarrow
ROH+KX
\]

---

# 3. Reduction of Aldehydes and Ketones

## Aldehyde:

\[
RCHO+H_2
\rightarrow
RCH_2OH
\]

---

## Ketone:

\[
RCOR'+H_2
\rightarrow
RCHOHR'
\]

---

# 4. Fermentation

Production of ethanol from sugar.

\[
C_6H_{12}O_6
\rightarrow
2C_2H_5OH+2CO_2
\]

Enzyme:

```
Zymase
```

---

# 5. Physical Properties of Alcohols

---

# 1. Hydrogen Bonding

Alcohol molecules form hydrogen bonds.

Effects:

- Higher boiling point
- Greater solubility

---

# 2. Solubility

Lower alcohols are soluble in water.

Solubility decreases with increasing carbon chain length.

---

# 6. Chemical Reactions of Alcohols

```
          Alcohol Reactions

                  │

 ┌────────────────┼────────────────┐

 ▼                ▼                ▼

Oxidation     Dehydration     Esterification
```

---

# 7. Reaction with Sodium

Alcohols react with sodium metal.

Reaction:

\[
2ROH+2Na
\rightarrow
2RONa+H_2
\]

Product:

Sodium alkoxide

---

# 8. Esterification

## Definition

Reaction of alcohol with carboxylic acid to form ester.

---

Reaction:

\[
RCOOH+ROH
\rightarrow
RCOOR+H_2O
\]

Catalyst:

\[
H_2SO_4
\]

---

Example:

Ethanol + Acetic acid

forms:

Ethyl acetate

---

# 9. Dehydration of Alcohols

Removal of water molecule.

---

Example:

\[
CH_3CH_2OH
\rightarrow
CH_2=CH_2+H_2O
\]

Reagent:

Concentrated H₂SO₄

---

# 10. Oxidation of Alcohols

---

# Primary Alcohol

Forms aldehyde then acid.

\[
RCH_2OH
\rightarrow
RCHO
\rightarrow
RCOOH
\]

---

# Secondary Alcohol

Forms ketone.

\[
R_2CHOH
\rightarrow
R_2CO
\]

---

# Tertiary Alcohol

Resistant to oxidation.

---

# 11. Phenols

## Definition

Phenols are compounds where hydroxyl group is directly attached to an aromatic ring.

General formula:

\[
Ar-OH
\]

---

Example:

Phenol:

\[
C_6H_5OH
\]

---

# 12. Preparation of Phenol

---

# 1. From Chlorobenzene

(Dow Process)

\[
C_6H_5Cl+NaOH
\rightarrow
C_6H_5OH+NaCl
\]

---

# 2. From Benzene Diazonium Salt

\[
C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-
\rightarrow
C_6H_5OH+N_2
\]

---

# 13. Physical Properties of Phenol

- Colourless solid/liquid
- Hydrogen bonding
- Slightly soluble in water

---

# 14. Acidity of Phenol

Phenol is more acidic than alcohol.

Reason:

Phenoxide ion is resonance stabilised.

---

Reaction:

\[
C_6H_5OH
\rightarrow
C_6H_5O^-+H^+
\]

---

# 15. Chemical Reactions of Phenol

---

# 1. Reaction with Sodium

\[
2C_6H_5OH+2Na
\rightarrow
2C_6H_5ONa+H_2
\]

---

# 2. Bromination

Phenol reacts with bromine water.

Reaction:

\[
C_6H_5OH+3Br_2
\rightarrow
C_6H_2Br_3OH+3HBr
\]

Product:

```
2,4,6-Tribromophenol
```

---

# 3. Nitration

Phenol forms nitrophenols.

---

# 4. Kolbe Reaction

Sodium phenoxide reacts with CO₂.

Product:

Salicylic acid

---

Reaction:

\[
C_6H_5ONa+CO_2
\rightarrow
C_6H_4(OH)COOH
\]

---

# 5. Reimer-Tiemann Reaction

Phenol reacts with chloroform and NaOH.

Product:

Salicylaldehyde

---

# 16. Ethers

## Definition

Ethers are compounds containing oxygen atom bonded to two alkyl or aryl groups.

General formula:

\[
R-O-R'
\]

---

Examples:

## Dimethyl ether

\[
CH_3OCH_3
\]

---

## Diethyl ether

\[
C_2H_5OC_2H_5
\]

---

# 17. Nomenclature of Ethers

Named as:

```
Alkoxy alkane
```

---

Example:

\[
CH_3OCH_2CH_3
\]

Name:

```
Methoxyethane
```

---

# 18. Preparation of Ethers

---

# Williamson Ether Synthesis

## Definition

Preparation of ethers by reaction of sodium alkoxide with alkyl halide.

---

Reaction:

\[
RONa+RX
\rightarrow
ROR+NaX
\]

---

Example:

\[
C_2H_5ONa+C_2H_5Br
\rightarrow
C_2H_5OC_2H_5
\]

---

# 19. Physical Properties of Ethers

- Lower boiling point than alcohols.
- Slightly soluble in water.
- Volatile liquids.

---

# 20. Chemical Reactions of Ethers

---

# Cleavage of Ethers

Ethers react with HI.

Reaction:

\[
ROR'+HI
\rightarrow
RI+R'OH
\]

---

# 21. Important Comparison

| ---------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| R-OH             | Ar-OH         | R-O-R         |
| Saturated carbon | Aromatic ring | Oxygen bridge |
| Less acidic      | More acidic   | Neutral       |

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. Why is phenol more acidic than alcohol?

**Answer:**

Phenoxide ion formed after removal of H⁺ is resonance stabilised, making phenol more acidic.

---

## 2. Explain Williamson synthesis.

**Answer:**

Williamson synthesis is the preparation of ethers by reacting sodium alkoxide with alkyl halides.

---

## 3. What happens when ethanol is heated with concentrated H₂SO₄?

**Answer:**

Ethanol undergoes dehydration to form ethene.

\[
C_2H_5OH
\rightarrow
C_2H_4+H_2O
\]

---

## 4. Explain Reimer-Tiemann reaction.

**Answer:**

Phenol reacts with chloroform and NaOH to form salicylaldehyde.

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing alcohol and phenol structures.
- Forgetting phenol acidity reason.
- Mixing oxidation products of primary and secondary alcohols.
- Writing wrong Williamson reaction conditions.
- Confusing ether cleavage products.
