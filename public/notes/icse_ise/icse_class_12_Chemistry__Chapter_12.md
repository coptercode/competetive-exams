# Introduction

**Diazonium salts** are aromatic compounds containing the diazonium group:

\[
-ArN_2^+
\]

They are important intermediates in the preparation of many aromatic compounds and dyes.

---

# 1. General Formula

\[
ArN_2^+X^-
\]

Where:

- Ar = Aromatic group
- X⁻ = Counter ion

---

# Example

## Benzene diazonium chloride

Formula:

\[
C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-
\]

---

# 2. Preparation of Diazonium Salts

The preparation of diazonium salts from primary aromatic amines is called:

## Diazotisation

---

# Diazotisation Reaction

## Definition

Conversion of primary aromatic amines into diazonium salts using nitrous acid at low temperature.

---

# General Reaction

\[
ArNH_2+NaNO_2+2HCl
\rightarrow
ArN_2^+Cl^-+NaCl+2H_2O
\]

---

# Conditions

Temperature:

\[
273-278K
\]

(0–5°C)

---

# Reagents

Nitrous acid is generated in situ:

\[
NaNO_2+HCl
\rightarrow
HNO_2+NaCl
\]

---

# Example

Aniline:

\[
C_6H_5NH_2
\]

↓

Benzene diazonium chloride:

\[
C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-
\]

---

# 3. Structure of Diazonium Group

The diazonium ion contains:

\[
N\equiv N^+
\]

---

Geometry:

```
Linear
```

Bond angle:

\[
180^\circ
\]

---

# 4. Stability of Diazonium Salts

Aryl diazonium salts are more stable than alkyl diazonium salts.

---

Reason:

Resonance stabilisation of aromatic ring.

---


# 6. Replacement Reactions

The diazonium group (-N₂⁺) is replaced by other groups.

---

# 1. Sandmeyer Reaction

## Definition

Replacement of diazonium group by Cl, Br, or CN using copper salts.

---

## Chlorobenzene Formation

\[
C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-+CuCl
\rightarrow
C_6H_5Cl+N_2
\]

---

## Bromobenzene Formation

\[
C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-+CuBr
\rightarrow
C_6H_5Br+N_2
\]

---

## Benzonitrile Formation

\[
C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-+CuCN
\rightarrow
C_6H_5CN+N_2
\]

---

# 7. Gattermann Reaction

## Definition

Replacement of diazonium group by halogen using copper powder and hydrochloric/hydrobromic acid.

---

Reaction:

\[
ArN_2^+Cl^-+Cu/HCl
\rightarrow
ArCl+N_2
\]

---

# 8. Replacement by Iodine

Diazonium salt reacts with potassium iodide.

Reaction:

\[
ArN_2^+Cl^-+KI
\rightarrow
ArI+N_2
\]

---

# 9. Replacement by Fluorine

Prepared through:

## Balz-Schiemann Reaction

---

Steps:

Diazonium salt reacts with fluoroboric acid.

\[
ArN_2^+BF_4^-
\]

On heating:

\[
ArF+BF_3+N_2
\]

---

# 10. Replacement by Hydroxyl Group

Diazonium salts on warming with water produce phenols.

Reaction:

\[
ArN_2^+Cl^-+H_2O
\rightarrow
ArOH+N_2+HCl
\]

---

# 11. Replacement by Hydrogen

Reduction gives benzene.

Reagent:

\[
H_3PO_2
\]

Reaction:

\[
ArN_2^+Cl^-
\rightarrow
ArH+N_2
\]

---

# 12. Coupling Reactions

## Definition

Diazonium salts react with activated aromatic compounds to form azo compounds.

---

General reaction:

\[
ArN_2^+Cl^-+Ar'H
\rightarrow
Ar-N=N-Ar'
\]

---

Product:

```
Azo compound
```

---

# 13. Azo Dyes

Compounds containing:

\[
-N=N-
\]

group are called azo dyes.

---

Examples:

- Methyl orange
- Orange dyes

---

# 14. Coupling with Phenol

Diazonium salt reacts with phenol in alkaline medium.

Product:

p-hydroxyazobenzene

---

Reaction:

\[
C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-+C_6H_5OH
\rightarrow
Azo\ dye
\]

---

# 15. Coupling with Aniline

Forms amino azo compounds.

---

# 16. Importance of Diazonium Salts

Diazonium salts are useful because aromatic compounds can be prepared that are difficult to obtain directly.

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. What is diazotisation?

**Answer:**

Diazotisation is the conversion of primary aromatic amines into diazonium salts using NaNO₂ and HCl at 273–278 K.

---

## 2. Explain Sandmeyer reaction.

**Answer:**

In Sandmeyer reaction, diazonium salts are converted into aryl halides or nitriles using copper salts.

---

## 3. Why are diazonium salts prepared at low temperature?

**Answer:**

Because diazonium salts are unstable at higher temperatures and may decompose.

---

## 4. What are azo dyes?

**Answer:**

Organic compounds containing the azo group (-N=N-) formed by coupling reactions of diazonium salts are called azo dyes.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Forgetting temperature requirement (0–5°C).
- Confusing Sandmeyer and Gattermann reactions.
- Writing alkyl diazonium salts as stable.
- Forgetting diazonium salts are mainly aromatic.
- Mixing azo dyes with ordinary dyes.

# Chapter Summary

- **Diazonium Salts** are important aromatic intermediates containing the -N₂⁺ group.
- They are prepared by diazotisation of primary aromatic amines at low temperature.
- Diazonium salts undergo replacement reactions to form halides, phenols, and nitriles.
- Coupling reactions produce azo dyes used in industries.
- Sandmeyer, Gattermann, and Balz-Schiemann reactions are important named reactions of diazonium salts.
