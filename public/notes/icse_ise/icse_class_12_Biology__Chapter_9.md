# Introduction

The increasing human population requires enhanced food production with improved quality.

**Strategies for enhancement in food production** involve scientific methods to increase:

- Crop yield
- Animal productivity
- Disease resistance
- Nutritional quality

---

# 1. Improvement in Crop Production

Crop improvement involves developing better plant varieties using:

- Plant breeding
- Genetic engineering
- Tissue culture

---

# 2. Plant Breeding

## Definition

Plant breeding is the science of improving crop varieties by changing their genetic composition.

---

# Objectives of Plant Breeding

1. Higher yield
2. Improved quality
3. Disease resistance
4. Resistance to environmental stress
5. Early maturity
6. Better adaptability

---

# 7. Tissue Culture

## Definition

Growing plant cells or tissues in an artificial nutrient medium under sterile conditions.

---

# Principle

Based on:

## Totipotency

Ability of a plant cell to develop into a complete plant.

---

# Process

```
Plant Tissue
      │
      ▼
Sterile Culture Medium
      │
      ▼
Callus Formation
      │
      ▼
Plant Regeneration
      │
      ▼
New Plant
```

---

# Applications

- Rapid multiplication
- Disease-free plants
- Production of rare plants
- Crop improvement

---

---

2. Cross-breeding

# 15. Controlled Breeding Techniques

---

# Artificial Insemination

## Definition

Introduction of semen into female reproductive tract artificially.

---

Advantages:

- Uses superior males.
- Improves livestock quality.

---

# Multiple Ovulation Embryo Transfer (MOET)

## Definition

A technique to produce many embryos from superior females.

---

# Steps

```
Hormonal Treatment
        ↓
Multiple Eggs Produced
        ↓
Fertilization
        ↓
Embryo Collection
        ↓
Transfer to Surrogate Mother
```

---

# Applications

- Rapid multiplication of superior animals.

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. What is plant breeding?

**Answer:**

Plant breeding is the science of improving crop varieties by selecting and crossing plants with desirable characteristics.

---

## 2. Explain tissue culture.

**Answer:**

Tissue culture is the growth of plant cells or tissues in an artificial nutrient medium under sterile conditions.

---

## 3. What is MOET?

**Answer:**

MOET is a reproductive technology where multiple embryos are produced from superior females and transferred to surrogate mothers.

---

## 4. Differentiate layers and broilers.

| Layers               | Broilers        |
| -------------------- | --------------- |
| Egg production       | Meat production |
| Longer growth period | Faster growth   |

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing hybridization and tissue culture.
- Mixing dairy farming and poultry farming.
- Forgetting totipotency principle.
- Confusing apiculture with aquaculture.
- Mixing layers and broilers.
