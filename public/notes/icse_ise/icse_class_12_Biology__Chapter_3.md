# Introduction

Human reproduction is a type of **sexual reproduction** involving the formation and fusion of male and female gametes.

It involves:

```
Formation of Gametes
        ↓
Transfer of Gametes
        ↓
Fertilization
        ↓
Embryonic Development
        ↓
Birth of New Individual
```

---

# 2. Testes

## Location

- Present in the scrotum.
- Located outside the abdominal cavity.

---

# Functions

### 1. Spermatogenesis

Production of sperms.

### 2. Hormone Secretion

Production of testosterone.

---

# Structure of Testis

Each testis contains:

- Seminiferous tubules
- Leydig cells
- Sertoli cells

---

# Seminiferous Tubules

Function:

- Formation of sperm cells.

Contain:

### Spermatogenic Cells

Develop into sperms.

---

### Sertoli Cells

Functions:

- Provide nutrition to developing sperm cells.
- Support spermatogenesis.

---

### Leydig Cells

Function:

- Secrete testosterone.

# 4. Accessory Glands

Three major glands:

1. Seminal vesicles
2. Prostate gland
3. Bulbourethral glands

---

# Seminal Vesicles

Secrete:

- Fructose-rich fluid.

Function:

- Provides energy to sperm.

---

# Prostate Gland

Secretes:

- Alkaline fluid.

Function:

- Protects sperm.

---

# Bulbourethral Glands

Function:

- Lubrication.

---

# Semen

Mixture of:

```
Sperms + Seminal Plasma
```

# Difference Between Spermatogenesis and Oogenesis

| Spermatogenesis       | Oogenesis           |
| --------------------- | ------------------- |
| Occurs in testes      | Occurs in ovaries   |
| Produces sperm        | Produces ovum       |
| Four functional cells | One functional ovum |
| Begins at puberty     | Begins before birth |

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. Explain spermatogenesis.

**Answer:**

Spermatogenesis is the process of formation of sperm from spermatogonia in seminiferous tubules of testes.

---

## 2. Where does fertilization occur in humans?

**Answer:**

Fertilization occurs at the ampullary-isthmic junction of the fallopian tube.

---

## 3. Write functions of placenta.

**Answer:**

Placenta provides nutrition, gas exchange, waste removal, and hormone production.

---

## 4. What is implantation?

**Answer:**

Implantation is the attachment of blastocyst to the uterine endometrium.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing ovulation with fertilization.
- Writing uterus as site of fertilization.
- Confusing sperm formation and maturation sites.
- Forgetting hormone functions.
- Mixing menstrual cycle phases.

# Chapter Summary

- Human reproduction involves specialized male and female reproductive systems.
- Spermatogenesis produces sperm, while oogenesis produces ova.
- The menstrual cycle prepares the female body for pregnancy.
- Fertilization forms a zygote, which develops into an embryo after implantation.
- The placenta supports fetal growth by providing nutrients and removing wastes.
- Parturition and lactation complete the reproductive process.
