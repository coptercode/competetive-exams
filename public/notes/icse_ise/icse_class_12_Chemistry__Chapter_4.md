# Introduction

---

# 1. d-Block Elements

## Definition

Elements in which the last electron enters the **d-orbital** are called d-block elements.

---

# Position

Located between:

```
s-block and p-block
```

---

# General Electronic Configuration

\[
(n-1)d^{1-10}ns^{0-2}
\]

---

# Transition Elements

## Definition

Elements that have partially filled d-orbitals in their atoms or in any oxidation state are called transition elements.

---

# Examples

| Element | Configuration |
| ------- | ------------- |
| Sc      | 3d¹4s²        |
| Ti      | 3d²4s²        |
| Fe      | 3d⁶4s²        |
| Cu      | 3d¹⁰4s¹       |

---

# 2. Characteristics of Transition Elements

Transition elements show:

- Magnetic properties
- Complex formation
- Catalytic activity
- Alloy formation

---

# 3. Electronic Configuration of d-Block Elements

General configuration:

\[
(n-1)d^{1-10}ns^{0-2}
\]

---

# Exceptions

## Chromium (Cr)

Expected:

\[
3d^4 4s^2
\]

Actual:

\[
3d^5 4s^1
\]

Reason:

Half-filled d-orbital stability.

---

## Copper (Cu)

Expected:

\[
3d^9 4s^2
\]

Actual:

\[
3d^{10}4s^1
\]

Reason:

Completely filled d-orbital stability.

---

Due to presence of:

```

```

---

# Types

## Paramagnetic

Contains unpaired electrons.

Example:

\[

\]

---

## Diamagnetic

\[

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. Why do transition metals show variable oxidation states?

**Answer:**

Because the energies of ns and (n−1)d orbitals are very close, electrons from both orbitals participate in bonding.

---

## 2. Why are transition metal compounds coloured?

**Answer:**

Due to d-d electronic transitions caused by absorption of visible light.

---

## 3. Explain lanthanide contraction.

**Answer:**

The gradual decrease in size of lanthanide ions from La to Lu due to poor shielding effect of 4f electrons is called lanthanide contraction.

---

## 4. Why are actinides radioactive?

**Answer:**

Actinides have unstable nuclei due to large atomic size and high number of protons.

# Common Mistakes

- Forgetting Cr and Cu exceptions.
- Mixing lanthanide and actinide configurations.
- Writing oxidation states incorrectly.
- Confusing paramagnetic and diamagnetic behaviour.

- **d- and f-block elements** explain the properties of transition and inner transition metals.
- Transition metals exhibit variable oxidation states, coloured compounds, magnetic behaviour, complex formation, and catalytic activity.
