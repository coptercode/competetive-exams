# Introduction

**Electromagnetic waves** are waves consisting of oscillating electric and magnetic fields that can travel through vacuum.

They were theoretically predicted by:

```
James Clerk Maxwell
```

and experimentally verified by:

```
Heinrich Hertz
```

---

# 1. Maxwell's Theory of Electromagnetic Waves

Maxwell proposed that:

1. A changing electric field produces a magnetic field.
2. A changing magnetic field produces an electric field.
3. These fields travel together as electromagnetic waves.

---

# 2. Production of Electromagnetic Waves

Electromagnetic waves are produced by:

```
Accelerated charges
```

Example:

- Oscillating electrons in antennas

---

# 3. Nature of Electromagnetic Waves

Electromagnetic waves are:

- Transverse waves
- Non-mechanical waves
- Do not require a medium

They can travel through:

```
Vacuum
```

---

# 4. Structure of Electromagnetic Wave

An electromagnetic wave consists of:

```
Electric Field (E)

        ↑

        │

        │

        ─────────→ Direction of propagation

        │

        ↓

Magnetic Field (B)
```

---

# Properties:

Electric field and magnetic field are:

- Mutually perpendicular
- Perpendicular to direction of propagation

Therefore:

\[
E \perp B \perp Direction
\]

---

# 5. Speed of Electromagnetic Waves

In vacuum:

\[
c=3\times10^8 ms^{-1}
\]

---

Relation:

\[
c=\frac1{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}
\]

Where:

- μ₀ = Permeability of free space
- ε₀ = Permittivity of free space

---

# 7. Electromagnetic Spectrum

The complete range of electromagnetic radiations arranged according to wavelength or frequency is called the electromagnetic spectrum.

---

Order:

```
Low Frequency                         High Frequency

Radio → Microwave → Infrared → Visible → UV → X-ray → Gamma

Long Wavelength                    Short Wavelength
```

# 16. Electromagnetic Energy

Energy of electromagnetic wave:

\[
E=h\nu
\]

Where:

- h = Planck's constant
- ν = Frequency

---

Since:

\[
\nu=\frac c\lambda
\]

Therefore:

\[
E=\frac{hc}{\lambda}
\]

---

# 17. Momentum of Electromagnetic Wave

Momentum:

\[
p=\frac Ec
\]

or

\[
p=\frac h\lambda
\]

# Board Important Questions

## 1. What are electromagnetic waves?

**Answer:**

Electromagnetic waves are waves consisting of oscillating electric and magnetic fields that propagate through space.

---

## 2. Write properties of electromagnetic waves.

**Answer:**

1. They are transverse waves.
2. They do not require a medium.
3. They travel with speed of light.
4. Electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular.

---

## 3. State Maxwell's prediction.

**Answer:**

Maxwell predicted that changing electric and magnetic fields produce electromagnetic waves travelling with the speed of light.

---

## 4. Arrange electromagnetic waves in increasing frequency order.

**Answer:**

\[
Radio < Microwave < Infrared < Visible < UV < X-ray < Gamma
\]

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing wavelength and frequency order.
- Forgetting electromagnetic waves are transverse.
- Mixing infrared and ultraviolet applications.
- Forgetting EM waves travel without medium.
- Confusing X-rays and gamma rays.
