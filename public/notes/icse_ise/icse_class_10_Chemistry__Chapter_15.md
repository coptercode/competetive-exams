# Introduction

Chemistry plays an important role in our daily lives. From the food we eat and the medicines we take to the fuels we use and the materials around us, almost everything involves chemical substances or reactions. Understanding these applications helps us appreciate how chemistry contributes to health, agriculture, industry, transportation, and environmental protection.

---

# 1. Chemistry in Food

## Importance

Chemicals are used in food preparation, preservation, and nutrition.

### Examples

- Common Salt (NaCl) – Food seasoning.
- Baking Soda (NaHCO₃) – Baking powder.
- Vinegar (CH₃COOH) – Food preservative.
- Citric Acid – Soft drinks and fruits.

# 9. Green Chemistry

## Definition

**Green Chemistry** is the design of chemical processes that reduce or eliminate the use and generation of hazardous substances.

---

## Principles

- Use safer chemicals.
- Save energy.
- Use renewable resources.

# Solved Examples

## Example 1

### Question

Why is ammonia important in agriculture?

### Solution

Ammonia is used to manufacture nitrogen fertilizers.

### Answer

It is used in the production of fertilizers that improve crop yield.

---

## Example 2

### Question

Name one chemical used in batteries.

### Solution

Lead-acid batteries contain a strong mineral acid.

### Answer

```
Sulphuric Acid (H₂SO₄)
```

---

## Example 3

### Question

Which organic compound is used as a sanitizer?

### Solution

Alcohols are widely used as disinfectants.

### Answer

```
Ethanol
```

---

## Example 4

### Question

What is Green Chemistry?

### Solution

It focuses on reducing harmful chemicals and pollution.

### Answer

The design of environmentally friendly chemical products and processes.

---

## Example 5

### Question

State one use of hydrochloric acid.

### Solution

Hydrochloric acid has several industrial applications.

### Answer

It is used for **cleaning metal surfaces** before electroplating.

# Common Mistakes

- Assuming chemistry is used only in laboratories.
- Confusing industrial uses of **H₂SO₄**, **HNO₃**, and **HCl**.
- Forgetting the importance of **Green Chemistry**.
- Mixing up the uses of ethanol and ethanoic acid.
- Ignoring the environmental impact of chemicals.

# Chapter Summary

- Chemistry is an essential part of everyday life, contributing to **food production, medicine, agriculture, transportation, construction, and environmental protection**.
- Important compounds such as **hydrochloric acid, ammonia, nitric acid, sulphuric acid, ethanol, and ethanoic acid** have numerous industrial and domestic applications.
- Metals and alloys are widely used in electrical wiring, vehicles, buildings, and medical instruments due to their useful physical and chemical properties.
- Modern chemistry also emphasizes **Green Chemistry**, which promotes sustainable practices by reducing pollution, conserving resources, and using safer chemicals.
- Understanding the applications of chemistry helps explain how scientific knowledge improves the quality of life while encouraging responsible use of chemicals to protect the environment.
