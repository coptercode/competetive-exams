# Introduction

**Ethanol (C₂H₅OH)** and **Ethanoic Acid (CH₃COOH)** are two of the most important organic compounds studied in chemistry. Ethanol is an **alcohol** containing the **hydroxyl (-OH)** functional group, while ethanoic acid is a **carboxylic acid** containing the **carboxyl (-COOH)** functional group. These compounds have numerous applications in industries, medicine, food preservation, fuel production, and laboratories.

---

# 1. Ethanol

## Definition

**Ethanol** is a colourless, volatile liquid belonging to the alcohol family. It contains the **hydroxyl (-OH)** functional group.

---

## Molecular Formula

```
C₂H₅OH
```

---

## Structural Formula

```
   H   H
   |   |
H–C–C–OH
   |   |
   H   H
```

---

## Functional Group

```
–OH (Hydroxyl Group)
```

---

# 2. Preparation of Ethanol

## (i) Fermentation of Sugar

Yeast converts sugar into ethanol and carbon dioxide under anaerobic conditions.

### Chemical Equation

```
C₆H₁₂O₆

Yeast

↓

2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂
```

---

## Conditions

- Temperature: **25–35°C**
- Absence of air.
- Presence of yeast.

---

## (ii) Hydration of Ethene (Industrial Method)

```
C₂H₄ + H₂O

↓

C₂H₅OH
```

---

# 3. Physical Properties of Ethanol

- Colourless liquid.
- Pleasant smell.
- Miscible with water.
- Neutral to litmus.
- Volatile.
- Good solvent.
- Boiling point: **78°C**

---

# 4. Chemical Properties of Ethanol

---

## (i) Combustion

```
C₂H₅OH + 3O₂

↓

2CO₂ + 3H₂O
```

### Observation

Burns with a **clean blue flame**, releasing heat.

---

## (ii) Reaction with Sodium

```
2C₂H₅OH + 2Na

↓

2C₂H₅ONa + H₂↑
```

### Observation

Hydrogen gas is evolved.

---

## (iii) Oxidation

```
C₂H₅OH + [O]

↓

CH₃COOH + H₂O
```

### Oxidizing Agent

- Acidified potassium dichromate (K₂Cr₂O₇)
- Acidified potassium permanganate (KMnO₄)

---

# 6. Ethanoic Acid

## Definition

**Ethanoic Acid** is a weak organic acid containing the **carboxyl (-COOH)** functional group.

---

## Molecular Formula

```
CH₃COOH
```

---

## Common Name

```
Acetic Acid
```

---

## Structural Formula

```
      O
      ||
CH₃ — C — OH
```

---

## Functional Group

```
–COOH (Carboxyl Group)
```

---

# 7. Preparation of Ethanoic Acid

## Oxidation of Ethanol

```
C₂H₅OH + [O]

↓

CH₃COOH + H₂O
```

---

## Industrial Method

Oxidation of acetaldehyde or methanol using suitable catalysts.

---

# 8. Physical Properties of Ethanoic Acid

- Colourless liquid.
- Pungent smell.
- Sour taste.
- Completely miscible with water.
- Freezes at **16.6°C** (Glacial Acetic Acid).
- Weak acid.

---

# 9. Chemical Properties of Ethanoic Acid

---

```

CH₃COO⁻ + H₃O⁺
```

---

## (ii) Reaction with Metals

```

Zn(CH₃COO)₂ + H₂↑
```

---

## (iii) Reaction with Carbonates

```
2CH₃COOH + Na₂CO₃

↓

2CH₃COONa + CO₂ + H₂O
```

---

## (iv) Reaction with Bases

```
CH₃COOH + NaOH

↓

CH₃COONa + H₂O
```

---

# 10. Esterification

## Definition

**Esterification** is the reaction between an alcohol and a carboxylic acid to form an **ester** and water in the presence of concentrated sulfuric acid.

---

## Chemical Equation

```
CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH

Conc. H₂SO₄

↓

CH₃COOC₂H₅ + H₂O
```

---

## Product

```
Ethyl Ethanoate
```

---

## Observation

Produces a **pleasant fruity smell**.

---

# 11. Saponification

## Definition

**Saponification** is the alkaline hydrolysis of an ester to produce an alcohol and the salt of a carboxylic acid (soap).

---

## Example

```
CH₃COOC₂H₅ + NaOH

↓

CH₃COONa + C₂H₅OH
```

---

# 12. Glacial Acetic Acid

## Definition

Pure ethanoic acid is known as **Glacial Acetic Acid**.

---

## Characteristics

- Freezes at **16.6°C**.
- Ice-like appearance.
- Highly concentrated.

---

# Difference Between Ethanol and Ethanoic Acid

| Ethanol               | Ethanoic Acid           |
| --------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Alcohol               | Carboxylic acid         |
| Formula: C₂H₅OH       | Formula: CH₃COOH        |
| Functional group: –OH | Functional group: –COOH |
| Neutral               | Weakly acidic           |
| Pleasant smell        | Vinegar-like smell      |

---

# Difference Between Fermentation and Esterification

| Fermentation              | Esterification                 |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Produces ethanol          | Produces esters                |
| Uses yeast                | Uses concentrated H₂SO₄        |
| Sugar is the raw material | Alcohol and acid are reactants |

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing **ethanol (C₂H₅OH)** with **ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH)**.
- Mixing up the **–OH** and **–COOH** functional groups.
- Forgetting that **concentrated sulfuric acid** acts as a catalyst in esterification.
- Assuming ethanol is acidic (it is neutral).
- Forgetting that **glacial acetic acid** is pure ethanoic acid.
- Confusing **esterification** with **saponification**.
- Writing incorrect products for oxidation of ethanol.

# Quick Revision

- Ethanol → **C₂H₅OH**.
- Functional group → **–OH**.
- Ethanoic Acid → **CH₃COOH**.
- Functional group → **–COOH**.
- Fermentation → Produces ethanol.
- Oxidation of ethanol → Ethanoic acid.
- Esterification → Alcohol + Acid → Ester + Water.
- Ester → Pleasant fruity smell.
- Saponification → Ester + Alkali → Alcohol + Salt.
- Glacial Acetic Acid → Pure ethanoic acid.

# Chapter Summary

- **Ethanol** is a colourless alcohol containing the **–OH functional group**, prepared by **fermentation of sugar** or **hydration of ethene**, and widely used as a solvent, fuel, disinfectant, and in the pharmaceutical industry.
- **Ethanoic acid (acetic acid)** is a weak carboxylic acid containing the **–COOH functional group**, prepared by the oxidation of ethanol and commonly found in vinegar.
- Ethanol undergoes **combustion, oxidation, and reaction with sodium**, while ethanoic acid reacts with **metals, bases, and carbonates** to form salts.
- **Esterification** is an important reaction in which ethanol and ethanoic acid react in the presence of **concentrated sulfuric acid** to form **ethyl ethanoate**, a compound with a pleasant fruity smell.
- **Saponification** is the reverse process, where an ester reacts with an alkali to produce an alcohol and a salt.
- Ethanol and ethanoic acid have widespread applications in **food, medicine, fuels, perfumes, textiles, pharmaceuticals, and chemical industries**, making them among the most important organic compounds studied in chemistry.
