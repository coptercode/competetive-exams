# Introduction

**Optical instruments** are devices that use lenses and mirrors to obtain enlarged, clear, and detailed images of objects.

Examples:

- Microscope
- Telescope
- Camera
- Eye
- Spectrometer

---

# 1. Human Eye

The human eye is a natural optical instrument.

Forms image on retina.

# 2. Accommodation of Eye

## Definition

The ability of the eye lens to change its focal length to see objects at different distances is called accommodation.

---

# Near Point

The minimum distance at which a normal eye can see clearly.

\[
D=25cm
\]

---

# Far Point

The maximum distance at which the eye can see clearly.

For normal eye:

\[
\infty
\]

---

# 3. Defects of Vision

Common defects:

1. Myopia
2. Hypermetropia
3. Presbyopia

---

# 4. Myopia (Short Sightedness)

## Definition

A person can see nearby objects clearly but distant objects appear blurred.

---

Cause:

Image forms:

```
Before retina
```

---

Correction:

Using:

```
Concave lens
```

---

Power:

Negative power lens.

---

# 5. Hypermetropia (Long Sightedness)

## Definition

A person can see distant objects clearly but nearby objects appear blurred.

---

Cause:

Image forms:

```
Behind retina
```

---

Correction:

Using:

```
Convex lens
```

---

# 6. Presbyopia

## Definition

Loss of accommodation power of eye due to ageing.

---

Cause:

Weakening of eye muscles.

---

Correction:

Using:

```
Bifocal lens
```

---

# 7. Simple Microscope

## Definition

A simple microscope is a single convex lens used to obtain a magnified image of small objects.

---

Examples:

- Magnifying glass
- Reading lens

---

# Principle

A convex lens forms a:

```
Virtual, erect, magnified image
```

---

# Magnifying Power

When final image is at near point:

\[
M=1+\frac{D}{f}
\]

---

When final image is at infinity:

\[
M=\frac{D}{f}
\]

---

Where:

- D = Least distance of distinct vision
- f = Focal length

---

# 8. Compound Microscope

## Definition

A microscope using two convex lenses is called a compound microscope.

---

Two lenses:

1. Objective lens
2. Eyepiece lens

---

# Construction

```
Object

 ↓

Objective Lens

 ↓

Intermediate Image

 ↓

Eyepiece

 ↓

Final Magnified Image
```

---

# Objective Lens

Characteristics:

- Short focal length
- Small aperture

---

# Eyepiece

Characteristics:

- Acts as magnifier
- Larger focal length than objective

---

# Magnifying Power of Compound Microscope

Final image at infinity:

\[
M=
\frac{L}{f_o}
\frac{D}{f_e}
\]

---

Where:

- L = Length of microscope tube
- fo = Focal length of objective
- fe = Focal length of eyepiece

---

# 9. Resolving Power of Microscope

## Definition

Ability of microscope to distinguish two closely placed objects separately.

---

Resolution:

Smaller distance between objects that can be distinguished.

---

# Factors Affecting Resolution

- Wavelength of light
- Numerical aperture

# 12. Magnifying Power of Telescope

For normal adjustment:

\[
M=
\frac{f_o}{f_e}
\]

---

Where:

- fo = Objective focal length
- fe = Eyepiece focal length

---

# 13. Telescope in Final Position

When final image is formed at infinity:

Condition:

\[
L=f_o+f_e
\]

---

# 18. Spherical Aberration

## Definition

Failure of rays from different parts of lens to meet at the same point.

---

Reduction:

- Using suitable lens combinations

| Microscope                   | Telescope                    |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Observes small objects       | Observes distant objects     |
| High magnification           | Large angular magnification  |
| Short focal length objective | Large focal length objective |

# Board Important Questions

## 1. Define accommodation of eye.

**Answer:**

Accommodation is the ability of the eye lens to change its focal length to focus objects at different distances.

---

## 2. Explain myopia and its correction.

**Answer:**

Myopia occurs when distant objects appear blurred because the image forms before the retina. It is corrected using a concave lens.

---

## 3. Derive magnifying power of telescope.

\[
M=\frac{f_o}{f_e}
\]

---

## 4. Explain working of compound microscope.

**Answer:**

The objective lens forms a magnified real image, which is further magnified by the eyepiece to produce the final virtual image.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing microscope and telescope formulas.
- Forgetting objective and eyepiece roles.
- Mixing myopia and hypermetropia corrections.
- Using wrong sign for focal length.
- Forgetting near point value.

# Chapter Summary

- **Optical Instruments** use lenses and mirrors to observe and analyse objects.
- The human eye works as a natural optical instrument.
- Defects like myopia, hypermetropia, and presbyopia can be corrected using suitable lenses.
- Microscopes are used for small objects, while telescopes are used for distant objects.
- Magnifying power determines the ability of optical instruments to enlarge images.
- Aberrations affect image quality and are corrected using special techniques.
