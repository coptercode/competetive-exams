# Introduction

Environmental issues are problems caused by natural processes and human activities that disturb ecological balance.

Major environmental problems include:

- Pollution
- Global warming
- Ozone depletion
- Waste accumulation
- Loss of biodiversity

---

# 1. Environmental Pollution

## Definition

# 2. Air Pollution

## Definition

Presence of harmful substances in the atmosphere causing damage to living organisms and environment.

---

# Major Air Pollutants

## 1. Carbon Monoxide (CO)

Source:

- Automobile exhaust
- Burning fuels

Effect:

- Reduces oxygen transport in blood.

---

## 2. Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

Source:

- Fossil fuel burning
- Deforestation

Effect:

- Global warming

---

## 3. Sulphur Dioxide (SO₂)

Source:

- Industries
- Burning coal

Effect:

- Acid rain
- Respiratory problems

---

## 4. Nitrogen Oxides (NOx)

Source:

- Vehicles
- Industries

Effect:

- Smog formation
- Acid rain

---

## 5. Particulate Matter

Examples:

- Dust
- Smoke
- Fine particles

Effects:

- Lung diseases
- Breathing problems

---

# 3. Control of Air Pollution

Methods:

- Use of cleaner fuels
- Plantation of trees

---

# Electrostatic Precipitator

## Definition

A device used to remove particulate pollutants from industrial exhaust.

---

# Working

```
Polluted Gas

     ↓

Electric Field

     ↓

Particles Charged

     ↓

Collected on Plates

     ↓

Clean Gas Released
```

## Definition

# Exam Important Questions

## 1. What causes ozone depletion?

**Answer:**

Ozone depletion is mainly caused by chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), which release chlorine radicals that destroy ozone molecules.

---

## 2. Define BOD.

**Answer:**

BOD is the amount of oxygen required by microorganisms to decompose organic matter present in water.

---

## 3. Explain biomagnification.

**Answer:**

Biomagnification is the increase in concentration of toxic substances at successive trophic levels of a food chain.

---

## 4. What is global warming?

**Answer:**

Global warming is the increase in Earth's average temperature due to accumulation of greenhouse gases.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing greenhouse effect with ozone depletion.
- Writing BOD as oxygen present instead of oxygen required.
- Mixing eutrophication and biomagnification.
- Forgetting CFCs as ozone-depleting substances.
- Confusing global warming and climate change.
