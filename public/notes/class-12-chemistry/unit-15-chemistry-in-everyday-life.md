# PART A: DRUGS AND MEDICINES

---

# 1. Drugs

## Definition

A drug is a chemical substance used for the diagnosis, prevention or treatment of diseases.

---

# 2. Classification of Drugs

Drugs are classified based on their action:

1. Analgesics
2. Antipyretics
3. Antibiotics
4. Antiseptics
5. Tranquillisers
6. Antacids

---

# PART B: ANALGESICS

---

# 3. Analgesics

## Definition

Drugs that reduce or relieve pain without causing loss of consciousness are called analgesics.

---

Types:

1. Non-narcotic analgesics
2. Narcotic analgesics

---

# 4. Non-Narcotic Analgesics

These relieve mild to moderate pain.

Examples:

- Aspirin
- Paracetamol

---

# Aspirin

Chemical name:

\[
\boxed{\text{Acetylsalicylic acid}}
\]

Formula:

\[
\boxed{C_9H_8O_4}
\]

Uses:

- Pain relief
- Fever reduction
- Blood clot prevention

---

# Paracetamol

Formula:

\[
\boxed{C_8H_9NO_2}
\]

Uses:

- Reduces fever
- Relieves mild pain

---

# 5. Narcotic Analgesics

Used for severe pain relief.

Examples:

- Morphine
- Heroin

---

Effects:

- Reduce severe pain
- May cause addiction

---

# PART C: ANTIPYRETICS

---

# 6. Antipyretics

## Definition

Drugs that reduce body temperature during fever are called antipyretics.

---

Examples:

- Aspirin
- Paracetamol

---

# PART D: ANTIBIOTICS

---

# 7. Antibiotics

## Definition

Chemical substances produced by microorganisms that inhibit or kill harmful microorganisms are called antibiotics.

---

Examples:

- Penicillin
- Streptomycin
- Tetracycline

---

# 8. Penicillin

Discovered by:

\[
\boxed{\text{Alexander Fleming}}
\]

Uses:

- Treatment of bacterial infections

---

# PART E: ANTISEPTICS AND DISINFECTANTS

---

# 9. Antiseptics

## Definition

Chemical substances applied to living tissues to prevent infection are called antiseptics.

---

Examples:

- Dettol
- Iodine solution
- Boric acid

---

# 10. Disinfectants

## Definition

Chemicals used on non-living surfaces to destroy microorganisms are called disinfectants.

---

Examples:

- Bleaching powder
- Chlorine
- Phenol

---

# Difference Between Antiseptics and Disinfectants

| Antiseptics            | Disinfectants              |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Used on living tissues | Used on non-living objects |
| Less toxic             | More concentrated          |
| Example: Iodine        | Example: Bleaching powder  |

---

---

# 11. Tranquillisers

## Definition

Drugs used to reduce anxiety, stress and mental tension are called tranquillisers.

---

Examples:

- Diazepam

---

Uses:

# PART G: ANTIACIDS

---

# 12. Antacids

## Definition

Substances that neutralise excess acid in the stomach are called antacids.

---

Examples:

- Magnesium hydroxide
- Aluminium hydroxide

---

Reaction:

\[
Mg(OH)_2+2HCl
\rightarrow
MgCl_2+2H_2O
\]

---

\]

\]

---

---

# PART J: ADDITION POLYMERS

---

# 16. Addition Polymerisation

## Definition

Formation of polymers by repeated addition of unsaturated monomers without elimination of small molecules.

---

Example:

Ethene:

\[
nCH_2=CH_2
\]

forms:

\[
\boxed{(-CH_2-CH_2-)_n}
\]

Polyethylene

---

# Important Addition Polymers

---

# Polyethylene (PE)

Monomer:

\[
Ethene
\]

Uses:

- Plastic bags
- Containers

---

# PVC

Full form:

\[
\boxed{\text{Polyvinyl chloride}}
\]

Monomer:

\[
CH_2=CHCl
\]

Uses:

- Pipes
- Insulation materials

---

# Teflon

Monomer:

\[
CF_2=CF_2
\]

Uses:

- Non-stick coatings

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing analgesics and antipyretics.
- Mixing antiseptics and disinfectants.
- Forgetting antibiotics act against microorganisms.
- Confusing addition and condensation polymerisation.
- Mixing monomers of PVC and Teflon.
- Forgetting polymer properties and applications.
- Confusing natural and synthetic polymers.

# Unit Summary

- Drugs help prevent and treat diseases.
- Analgesics reduce pain.
- Antipyretics reduce fever.
- Antiseptics are used on living tissues.
- Disinfectants are used on non-living surfaces.
- Polymers are large molecules made from monomers.
- Addition polymers form without elimination of small molecules.
- Condensation polymers form with elimination of small molecules.
