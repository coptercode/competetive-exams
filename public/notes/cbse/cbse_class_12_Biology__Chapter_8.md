# Introduction

Microorganisms are microscopic organisms that include:

- Bacteria
- Fungi
- Protozoans
- Algae
- Viruses

Although some microbes cause diseases, many microbes are highly beneficial and are used in:

- Food production
- Medicine
- Industry
- Agriculture
- Environmental management

The use of microbes for human benefit is called **microbial biotechnology**.

---

# 1. Microbes in Household Products

Microbes have been used traditionally in the preparation of food products.

---

# A. Curd Formation

## Microorganism

```
Lactobacillus
```

(also called lactic acid bacteria)

---

## Process

Lactobacillus converts milk sugar (lactose) into lactic acid.

```
Lactose

↓

Lactic Acid

↓

Milk Protein Coagulation

↓

Curd
```

---

## Benefits

- Improves nutritional quality.
- Increases vitamin B12 content.
- Improves digestion.
- Maintains intestinal health.

---

# B. Dough Fermentation

## Microorganism

```
Saccharomyces cerevisiae
```

(Baker's yeast)

---

## Uses

Used in preparation of:

- Bread
- Cakes
- Fermented foods

---

## Process

Yeast converts sugar into:

- Carbon dioxide
- Alcohol

```
Sugar

↓

CO₂ + Alcohol
```

CO₂ causes dough to rise.

# 3. Microbes in Sewage Treatment

Sewage contains:

- Organic matter
- Pathogens
- Pollutants

Treatment reduces environmental pollution.

---

# Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)

Two major stages:

1. Primary treatment
2. Secondary treatment

---

# A. Primary Treatment

## Physical Process

Includes:

- Filtration
- Removal of large particles
- Sedimentation

---

## Product

Forms:

```
Primary sludge
```

---

# B. Secondary Treatment

Also called:

```
Biological treatment
```

---

## Process

Microorganisms degrade organic matter.

---

## Activated Sludge

Air is pumped into sewage to promote growth of aerobic microbes.

---

## Microbial Flocs

Clusters of:

- Bacteria
- Fungi

attached with organic matter.

---

## Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)

## Definition

Amount of oxygen required by microorganisms to oxidize organic matter in water.

---

### Importance

High BOD indicates:

- More organic pollution.

Low BOD indicates:

- Cleaner water.

---

# Solved Examples

### Example 1

**Question:** Which microorganism is responsible for curd formation?

**Solution:**

Curd formation occurs due to **Lactobacillus**, which converts lactose in milk into lactic acid, causing milk proteins to coagulate and form curd.

---

### Example 2

**Question:** Why is activated sludge important in sewage treatment?

**Solution:**

Activated sludge contains microbial flocs that degrade organic matter present in sewage. This reduces BOD and produces cleaner wastewater.

---

### Example 3

**Question:** Name the microorganism used in bread making.

**Solution:**

The yeast **Saccharomyces cerevisiae** is used in bread making. It produces carbon dioxide during fermentation, causing dough to rise.

---

### Example 4

**Question:** What are methanogens? Give one example.

**Solution:**

Methanogens are anaerobic microorganisms that produce methane during decomposition of organic matter. Example:

```
Methanobacterium
```

---

### Example 5

**Question:** Explain the role of Rhizobium as a biofertilizer.

**Solution:**

Rhizobium lives in root nodules of leguminous plants and fixes atmospheric nitrogen into usable nitrogen compounds, increasing soil fertility naturally.

---

### Example 6

**Question:** Why are baculoviruses considered good biocontrol agents?

**Solution:**

Baculoviruses are highly specific to particular insect pests and do not harm beneficial insects, making them environmentally safe alternatives to chemical pesticides.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing **Lactobacillus** with **Saccharomyces**; Lactobacillus forms curd, while Saccharomyces is used in bread and alcohol fermentation.
- Forgetting that sewage treatment involves microorganisms mainly during the **secondary treatment stage**.
- Confusing **BOD** with the amount of oxygen present in water; BOD measures oxygen required by microbes to degrade organic matter.
- Assuming all bacteria are harmful; many bacteria are useful in food production, medicine, and agriculture.
- Mixing up **methanogens** with ordinary decomposer bacteria; methanogens specifically produce methane under anaerobic conditions.
- Confusing biofertilizers with chemical fertilizers; biofertilizers contain living organisms that improve soil fertility naturally.
- Forgetting that mycorrhiza helps plants mainly by improving **phosphorus absorption**.
- Assuming Bt toxin kills all insects; Bt is specific to certain insect groups.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Microbes play important roles in household products, industries, agriculture, medicine, and environmental management.
- **Lactobacillus** helps in curd formation, while **Saccharomyces cerevisiae** is used in baking and alcohol production.
- Microbes produce valuable industrial products such as antibiotics, organic acids, enzymes, and bioactive molecules.
- Sewage treatment uses microbial activity to reduce organic pollutants and lower **BOD**.
- **Methanogens** produce methane-rich biogas from organic waste under anaerobic conditions.
- Biological control agents such as **Bacillus thuringiensis**, **Trichoderma**, and **baculoviruses** provide eco-friendly pest management.
- Biofertilizers like **Rhizobium**, cyanobacteria, and mycorrhiza improve soil fertility and agricultural productivity.
- Microbial biotechnology demonstrates how microorganisms can be used sustainably for human welfare and environmental protection.
