# Introduction

**Biotechnology** is the branch of science that uses living organisms, cells, enzymes, or biological systems to develop useful products and processes for human welfare.

Modern biotechnology mainly involves:

- Genetic engineering
- Recombinant DNA technology
- Tissue culture technology

The two major principles of biotechnology are:

1. **Genetic engineering**
2. **Bioprocess engineering**

---

# 1. Principles of Biotechnology

## Definition of Genetic Engineering

Genetic engineering is the deliberate modification of an organism's genetic material by introducing, removing, or altering DNA sequences.

---

## Recombinant DNA Technology

## Definition

The technology of joining DNA molecules from two different sources and introducing them into a host organism is called recombinant DNA technology.

# 3. Restriction Enzymes

## Definition

Restriction enzymes are molecular scissors that cut DNA at specific sequences called recognition sites.

---

## Discovery

Discovered in bacteria as a defense mechanism against viruses.

---

# Types of Restriction Enzymes

## A. Exonucleases

Remove nucleotides from the ends of DNA.

```
DNA →→→ Removal from ends
```

---

## B. Endonucleases

Cut DNA within the molecule.

```
DNA

───✂───

Internal cutting
```

---

# 5. Recognition Sequence

Restriction enzymes recognize specific palindromic sequences.

Example:

EcoRI recognizes:

```
5' GAATTC 3'

3' CTTAAG 5'
```

---

# 6. Sticky Ends

Restriction enzymes often produce single-stranded overhanging ends.

Example:

```
5' G      AATTC 3'

3' CTTAA      G 5'
```

These sticky ends help DNA fragments join easily.

---

# 15. Steps of PCR

PCR occurs in three main stages:

---

# A. Denaturation

Temperature:

```
94–95°C
```

DNA double strand separates.

```
DNA

↓

Two single strands
```

---

# B. Annealing

Temperature:

```
50–60°C
```

Primers attach to complementary sequences.

---

# C. Extension

Temperature:

```
72°C
```

DNA polymerase synthesizes new DNA strands.

---

# PCR Cycle

```
Denaturation

↓

Annealing

↓

Extension

↓

Repeated Cycles

↓

Millions of DNA Copies
```

---

# Solved Examples

### Example 1

**Question:** What are restriction enzymes?

**Solution:**

Restriction enzymes are DNA-cutting enzymes that recognize specific nucleotide sequences and cleave DNA at those sites. They are essential tools in recombinant DNA technology.

---

### Example 2

**Question:** Why is Taq polymerase used in PCR?

**Solution:**

Taq polymerase is obtained from _Thermus aquaticus_ and remains stable at high temperatures used during DNA denaturation, making it suitable for repeated PCR cycles.

---

### Example 3

**Question:** What is the function of a cloning vector?

**Solution:**

A cloning vector carries foreign DNA into a host cell and allows replication and expression of the inserted gene.

---

### Example 4

**Question:** Explain sticky ends.

**Solution:**

Sticky ends are single-stranded overhangs produced when restriction enzymes cut DNA at specific sites. They allow complementary DNA fragments to join easily through hydrogen bonding and DNA ligase action.

---

### Example 5

**Question:** What is the role of selectable markers?

**Solution:**

Selectable markers help identify cells that have successfully received recombinant DNA. Antibiotic resistance genes are commonly used as selectable markers.

---

### Example 6

**Question:** Why is downstream processing important?

**Solution:**

Downstream processing removes impurities, purifies the desired product, and prepares it for safe commercial use.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing restriction endonucleases with exonucleases; endonucleases cut within DNA, while exonucleases remove nucleotides from ends.
- Forgetting that sticky ends are produced by specific restriction enzymes and help DNA fragments join.
- Confusing PCR with natural DNA replication; PCR occurs artificially in vitro.
- Assuming all vectors are plasmids; bacteriophages and artificial chromosomes can also act as vectors.
- Forgetting that **Taq polymerase** comes from _Thermus aquaticus_.
- Mixing up the steps of PCR; the correct order is **Denaturation → Annealing → Extension**.
- Confusing transformation with translation; transformation is DNA uptake by cells, while translation is protein synthesis.
- Ignoring the importance of downstream processing in commercial biotechnology.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Biotechnology uses living systems and biological molecules to develop useful products and processes.
- **Recombinant DNA technology** involves isolation, cutting, joining, and introduction of DNA into host cells.
- Restriction enzymes act as molecular scissors, while DNA ligase joins DNA fragments.
- Cloning vectors such as **plasmids (pBR322)** carry foreign DNA into host organisms.
- PCR amplifies specific DNA sequences through **denaturation, annealing, and extension** using thermostable **Taq polymerase**.
- Gel electrophoresis separates DNA fragments based on size, allowing isolation of desired DNA.
- Bioreactors enable large-scale production of biological products under controlled conditions.
- Downstream processing ensures purification, quality control, and preparation of biotechnology products for use.
- Genetic engineering has transformed medicine, agriculture, industry, and environmental management by enabling precise manipulation of genetic material.
