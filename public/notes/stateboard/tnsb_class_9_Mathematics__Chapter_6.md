# Introduction

Trigonometry is the branch of mathematics that studies the relationship between angles and sides of triangles.

This chapter covers:

- Trigonometric ratios
- Sine, cosine and tangent
- Reciprocal ratios
- Special angle values
- Complementary angles

---

# PART A: BASIC CONCEPTS OF TRIGONOMETRY

---

# 1. Right Angled Triangle

## Definition

A triangle containing one angle equal to:

\[
\boxed{90^\circ}
\]

is called a right-angled triangle.

---

Parts of a right triangle:

1. Hypotenuse
2. Opposite side
3. Adjacent side

---

# 2. Hypotenuse

The side opposite to the right angle is called the hypotenuse.

Properties:

- Longest side of the triangle.
- Always opposite to 90° angle.

---

# 3. Opposite Side

The side opposite to the angle being considered is called the opposite side.

---

# 4. Adjacent Side

The side next to the angle being considered (other than hypotenuse) is called the adjacent side.

---

# PART B: TRIGONOMETRIC RATIOS

---

# 5. Trigonometric Ratios

The ratios between sides of a right triangle are called trigonometric ratios.

---

There are six ratios:

1. Sine
2. Cosine
3. Tangent
4. Cosecant
5. Secant
6. Cotangent

---

# 6. Sine Ratio

Symbol:

\[
\boxed{\sin\theta}
\]

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
\sin\theta=
\frac{\text{Opposite side}}
{\text{Hypotenuse}}
}
\]

---

# 7. Cosine Ratio

Symbol:

\[
\boxed{\cos\theta}
\]

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
\cos\theta=
\frac{\text{Adjacent side}}
{\text{Hypotenuse}}
}
\]

---

# 8. Tangent Ratio

Symbol:

\[
\boxed{\tan\theta}
\]

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
\tan\theta=
\frac{\text{Opposite side}}
{\text{Adjacent side}}
}
\]

---

# 9. Cosecant Ratio

Symbol:

\[
\boxed{\cosec\theta}
\]

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
\cosec\theta=
\frac{\text{Hypotenuse}}
{\text{Opposite side}}
}
\]

---

# 10. Secant Ratio

Symbol:

\[
\boxed{\sec\theta}
\]

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
\sec\theta=
\frac{\text{Hypotenuse}}
{\text{Adjacent side}}
}
\]

---

# 11. Cotangent Ratio

Symbol:

\[
\boxed{\cot\theta}
\]

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
\cot\theta=
\frac{\text{Adjacent side}}
{\text{Opposite side}}
}
\]

---

# PART C: RECIPROCAL RELATIONSHIPS

---

\[
\boxed{
\sin\theta=\frac1{\cosec\theta}
}
\]

---

\[
\boxed{
\cos\theta=\frac1{\sec\theta}
}
\]

---

\[
\boxed{
\tan\theta=\frac1{\cot\theta}
}
\]

---

# PART D: TRIGONOMETRIC IDENTITIES

---

# 12. Pythagorean Identities

---

## Identity 1

\[
\boxed{
\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1
}
\]

---

## Identity 2

\[
\boxed{
1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta
}
\]

---

## Identity 3

\[
\boxed{
1+\cot^2\theta=\cosec^2\theta
}
\]

# PART F: COMPLEMENTARY ANGLES

---

# 17. Complementary Angles

## Definition

Two angles whose sum is:

\[
\boxed{90^\circ}
\]

are called complementary angles.

---

Example:

\[
30^\circ+60^\circ=90^\circ
\]

---

# 18. Complementary Angle Relations

---

\[
\boxed{
\sin(90^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta
}
\]

---

\[
\boxed{
\cos(90^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta
}
\]

---

\[
\boxed{
\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=\cot\theta
}
\]

---

\[
\boxed{
\cot(90^\circ-\theta)=\tan\theta
}
\]

---

\[
\boxed{
\sec(90^\circ-\theta)=\cosec\theta
}
\]

---

\[
\boxed{
\cosec(90^\circ-\theta)=\sec\theta
}
\]

# PART G: APPLICATIONS OF TRIGONOMETRY

---

# 19. Finding Unknown Sides

If one side and one angle are known, trigonometric ratios help find unknown sides.

---

Example:

\[
\sin\theta=\frac{Opposite}{Hypotenuse}
\]

---

# 20. Finding Unknown Angles

Using inverse trigonometric ratios:

\[
\theta=\sin^{-1}(value)
\]

\[
\theta=\cos^{-1}(value)
\]

\[
\theta=\tan^{-1}(value)
\]

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing opposite and adjacent sides.
- Forgetting hypotenuse is always opposite 90°.
- Mixing sin and cosec values.
- Forgetting tan 90° is not defined.
- Using wrong complementary angle relation.
