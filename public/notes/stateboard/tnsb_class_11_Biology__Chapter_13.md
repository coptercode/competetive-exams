> **Subject:** Biology  
> **Class:** 11  
> **Chapter:** Plant Growth and Development

# Introduction

Plant growth and development involve a series of changes that occur from seed germination to maturity. These processes are controlled by internal factors and plant growth regulators.

This chapter covers:

- Growth phases in plants
- Growth rate
- Conditions affecting growth
- Differentiation
- Plant growth regulators
- Auxin
- Gibberellin
- Cytokinin
- Ethylene
- Abscisic acid

---

# PART A: PLANT GROWTH

---

# 1. Growth

## Definition

Growth is an irreversible increase in size, volume, dry weight or number of cells of an organism.

---

Characteristics of growth:

- Permanent increase.
- Requires energy.
- Involves cell division and enlargement.

---

# 2. Phases of Plant Growth

Plant growth occurs in three phases:

1. Cell division
2. Cell elongation
3. Cell maturation

---

# 3. Cell Division Phase

Location:

\[
\boxed{\text{Meristematic regions}}
\]

---

Features:

- Cells divide actively.
- Cells are small.
- Dense cytoplasm present.

---

# 4. Cell Elongation Phase

Features:

- Cells increase in size.
- Vacuoles enlarge.
- Cell wall expands.

---

# 5. Cell Maturation Phase

Features:

- Cells become specialised.
- Develop specific functions.

---

# PART B: GROWTH RATE

---

# 6. Growth Rate

## Definition

The increase in growth per unit time is called growth rate.

---

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
Growth\ rate=
\frac{Increase\ in\ growth}{Time}
}
\]

---

# 7. Types of Growth Rate

---

## Arithmetic Growth

Growth occurs at a constant rate.

---

## Geometric Growth

Growth occurs rapidly due to continuous cell division.

---

# 8. Sigmoid Growth Curve

Plant growth generally follows an S-shaped curve.

Stages:

1. Lag phase
2. Log phase
3. Stationary phase

---

# PART C: CONDITIONS FOR PLANT GROWTH

---

# 9. Factors Affecting Growth

---

## 1. Water

Importance:

- Essential for photosynthesis.
- Maintains cell expansion.

---

## 2. Minerals

Required for:

- Enzyme activity.
- Plant metabolism.

---

## 3. Temperature

Affects:

- Enzyme function.
- Metabolic activities.

---

## 4. Light

Influences:

- Photosynthesis.
- Development.

---

## 5. Oxygen

Required for:

- Respiration.
- Energy production.

---

# PART D: DEVELOPMENTAL PROCESSES

---

# 10. Development

## Definition

Development includes all changes that occur during the life cycle of a plant.

---

It includes:

- Growth
- Differentiation
- Maturation
- Senescence

---

# 11. Differentiation

## Definition

The process by which unspecialised cells become specialised cells is called differentiation.

---

Example:

Meristematic cells → Xylem cells

---

# 12. Dedifferentiation

## Definition

The process by which mature cells regain the ability to divide is called dedifferentiation.

---

Example:

Formation of cambium from mature cells.

---

# 13. Redifferentiation

## Definition

The process by which dedifferentiated cells become specialised again is called redifferentiation.

---

# PART E: PLANT GROWTH REGULATORS

---

# 14. Plant Growth Regulators (PGRs)

## Definition

Chemical substances produced naturally in plants that regulate growth and development are called plant growth regulators.

---

Major PGRs:

1. Auxins
2. Gibberellins
3. Cytokinins
4. Ethylene
5. Abscisic acid

---

# PART F: AUXIN

---

# 15. Auxin

## Discovery

Discovered through experiments by:

\[
\boxed{\text{Charles Darwin and F.W. Went}}
\]

---

Examples:

- IAA (Indole Acetic Acid)

---

# Functions of Auxin

- Promotes cell elongation.
- Maintains apical dominance.
- Helps root formation.
- Promotes fruit development.

---

Agricultural applications:

- Rooting of cuttings.
- Weed control.

---

# PART G: GIBBERELLIN

---

# 16. Gibberellins

## Definition

Plant hormones that promote stem elongation are called gibberellins.

---

Example:

\[
\boxed{GA_3}
\]

---

Functions:

- Increases plant height.
- Breaks seed dormancy.
- Promotes flowering.
- Increases fruit size.

---

Agricultural applications:

- Increase sugarcane growth.
- Improve fruit production.

---

# PART H: CYTOKININ

---

# 17. Cytokinins

## Definition

Plant hormones that promote cell division are called cytokinins.

---

Functions:

- Stimulate cell division.
- Delay ageing of leaves.
- Promote shoot formation.

---

Example:

Kinetin

---

# PART I: ETHYLENE

---

# 18. Ethylene

## Definition

Ethylene is a gaseous plant hormone involved mainly in fruit ripening.

---

Functions:

- Promotes fruit ripening.
- Causes leaf and fruit fall.
- Helps flowering in some plants.

---

Applications:

- Artificial ripening of fruits.

---

# PART J: ABSCISIC ACID (ABA)

---

# 19. Abscisic Acid

## Definition

ABA is a plant hormone that generally inhibits growth.

---

Called:

\[
\boxed{\text{Stress hormone}}
\]

---

Functions:

- Induces seed dormancy.
- Causes stomatal closure.
- Helps plants tolerate stress.

---

# PART K: APPLICATIONS OF PGRs

---

# 20. Agricultural Uses

---

## Auxins

- Root development.
- Weed control.

---

## Gibberellins

- Increase fruit size.
- Increase stem growth.

---

## Cytokinins

- Delay ageing.
- Tissue culture.

---

## Ethylene

- Fruit ripening.

---

## ABA

- Stress resistance.

---

# Important Differences

## Auxin vs Cytokinin

| Auxin                    | Cytokinin              |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| Promotes cell elongation | Promotes cell division |
| Helps root formation     | Helps shoot formation  |

---

## Growth Promoter vs Growth Inhibitor

| Growth Promoters | Growth Inhibitors  |
| ---------------- | ------------------ |
| Auxin            | ABA                |
| Gibberellin      | Slows growth       |
| Cytokinin        | Maintains dormancy |

---

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing auxin and cytokinin functions.
- Forgetting ethylene is a gaseous hormone.
- Mixing growth promoters and inhibitors.
- Forgetting ABA causes stomatal closure.
- Confusing differentiation and dedifferentiation.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Plant growth involves division, elongation and maturation.
- Development includes growth and differentiation.
- Plant growth regulators control plant activities.
- Auxins promote elongation.
- Gibberellins promote stem growth.
- Cytokinins promote cell division.
- Ethylene promotes fruit ripening.
- ABA helps plants respond to stress.

\[
\boxed{
\text{Plant growth and development are controlled by internal hormones and external factors.}
}
\]
