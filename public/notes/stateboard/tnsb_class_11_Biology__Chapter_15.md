# Introduction

Transport of substances like oxygen, nutrients, hormones and waste products is essential for survival. In humans, this function is performed by blood and the circulatory system.

This chapter covers:

- Blood and lymph
- Blood groups
- Blood coagulation
- Human circulatory system
- Structure of heart
- Cardiac cycle
- ECG
- Double circulation
- Circulatory disorders

---

# PART A: BODY FLUIDS

---

# 1. Body Fluids

## Definition

The fluids present inside the body that transport substances are called body fluids.

---

Major body fluids:

1. Blood
2. Lymph

---

# PART B: BLOOD

---

# 2. Blood

## Definition

Blood is a fluid connective tissue that transports gases, nutrients, hormones and waste products.

---

Components of blood:

1. Plasma
2. Formed elements

---

# 3. Plasma

## Definition

The liquid component of blood is called plasma.

---

Composition:

- Water (about 90%)
- Proteins
- Salts
- Nutrients
- Hormones
- Waste materials

---

# 4. Plasma Proteins

Important plasma proteins:

---

## Albumin

Function:

- Maintains osmotic pressure.

---

## Globulins

Function:

- Immunity and defence.

---

## Fibrinogen

Function:

- Blood clotting.

# 8. Platelets

# PART E: BLOOD COAGULATION

---

# 15. Blood Clotting

## Definition

The process of preventing blood loss by formation of a clot is called coagulation.

---

# 16. Mechanism of Clotting

Steps:

1. Injury occurs.
2. Platelets release clotting factors.
3. Prothrombin changes into thrombin.
4. Fibrinogen changes into fibrin.
5. Fibrin forms clot.

---

Important:

\[
\boxed{
Fibrinogen \rightarrow Fibrin
}
\]

---

# PART F: LYMPH

---

# 17. Lymph

## Definition

A colourless fluid present in lymph vessels is called lymph.

---

Composition:

- Plasma
- White blood cells

---

Functions:

- Helps immunity.
- Transports absorbed fats.

# PART H: HUMAN HEART

---

# 19. Heart

## Definition

The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood throughout the body.

---

Location:

\[
\boxed{\text{Thoracic cavity}}
\]

---

# 20. Chambers of Heart

Human heart has four chambers:

1. Right atrium
2. Right ventricle
3. Left atrium
4. Left ventricle

---

# 21. Valves of Heart

Functions:

- Prevent backflow of blood.

---

Major valves:

## Tricuspid valve

Between:

Right atrium and right ventricle

---

## Bicuspid/Mitral valve

Between:

Left atrium and left ventricle

---

## Semilunar valves

Present at arteries.

---

# PART I: BLOOD VESSELS

---

# 22. Arteries

Characteristics:

- Carry blood away from heart.
- Thick muscular walls.
- High pressure.

---

Example:

Aorta

---

# 23. Veins

Characteristics:

- Carry blood towards heart.
- Thin walls.
- Valves present.

---

# 24. Capillaries

Characteristics:

- Smallest blood vessels.
- Allow exchange of substances.

---

# PART J: CARDIAC CYCLE

---

# 25. Cardiac Cycle

## Definition

The sequence of events during one complete heartbeat is called cardiac cycle.

---

Duration:

\[
\boxed{0.8\ seconds}
\]

---

Stages:

1. Atrial systole
2. Ventricular systole
3. Complete cardiac diastole

---

# 26. Heart Sounds

---

## First Heart Sound

"Lub"

Due to:

Closing of atrioventricular valves.

---

## Second Heart Sound

"Dub"

Due to:

Closing of semilunar valves.

---

# PART K: CARDIAC OUTPUT

---

## Definition

The amount of blood pumped by one ventricle per minute is called cardiac output.

---

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
Cardiac\ Output =
Heart\ Rate \times Stroke\ Volume
}
\]

---

Normal value:

\[
\approx 5\ litres/minute
\]

# PART M: DOUBLE CIRCULATION

---

# 30. Double Circulation

## Definition

The circulation in which blood passes through the heart twice during one complete cycle is called double circulation.

---

Types:

1. Pulmonary circulation
2. Systemic circulation

---

# 31. Pulmonary Circulation

Heart → Lungs → Heart

---

Function:

Oxygenation of blood.

---

# 32. Systemic Circulation

Heart → Body tissues → Heart

---

Function:

Supply oxygen and nutrients.

---

# Important Differences

## Arteries vs Veins

| Arteries        | Veins          |
| --------------- | -------------- |
| Away from heart | Towards heart  |
| Thick walls     | Thin walls     |
| No valves       | Valves present |

---

## Blood vs Lymph

| Blood            | Lymph                |
| ---------------- | -------------------- |
| Red coloured     | Colourless           |
| Contains RBCs    | Mainly WBCs          |
| Faster transport | Returns tissue fluid |

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing arteries and veins.
- Forgetting RBCs contain haemoglobin.
- Mixing pulmonary and systemic circulation.
- Forgetting fibrin is responsible for clot formation.
- Confusing ECG waves.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Blood and lymph are important body fluids.
- Blood consists of plasma, RBCs, WBCs and platelets.
- Heart pumps blood through blood vessels.
- Cardiac cycle controls heartbeat.
- ECG records electrical activity of heart.
- Double circulation ensures efficient oxygen supply.
- Circulatory disorders affect heart and blood vessels.

\[
\boxed{

}
