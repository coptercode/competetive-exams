# Introduction

Mensuration is the branch of mathematics that deals with measurement of geometric figures such as length, area, surface area and volume.

This chapter covers:

- Heron's formula
- Area of triangles using side lengths
- Surface area of cube and cuboid
- Volume and capacity calculations

---

# PART A: HERON'S FORMULA

---

# 1. Area of Triangle

The area of a triangle can be found using:

\[
\boxed{
Area=\frac12 \times base \times height
}
\]

---

But when all three sides are known, Heron's formula is used.

---

# 2. Heron's Formula

## Definition

Heron's formula is used to find the area of a triangle when the lengths of all three sides are known.

---

Let sides of triangle be:

\[
a,\ b,\ c
\]

---

# 3. Semi-Perimeter

## Definition

Half of the perimeter of a triangle is called semi-perimeter.

---

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}
}
\]

---

# 4. Heron's Formula

\[
\boxed{
Area=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}
}
\]

---

Where:

s = semi-perimeter

a, b, c = sides of triangle

---

# Example

Find area of a triangle with sides:

\[
5cm,\ 6cm,\ 7cm
\]

---

Step 1:

Find semi-perimeter:

\[
s=\frac{5+6+7}{2}
\]

\[
s=\frac{18}{2}=9
\]

---

Step 2:

Apply Heron's formula:

\[
Area=\sqrt{9(9-5)(9-6)(9-7)}
\]

\[
=\sqrt{9\times4\times3\times2}
\]

\[
=\sqrt{216}
\]

\[
\boxed{Area=6\sqrt6\ cm^2}
\]

---

# PART B: SURFACE AREA OF CUBOID

---

# 5. Cuboid

## Definition

A solid figure having six rectangular faces is called a cuboid.

---

Dimensions:

- Length (l)
- Breadth (b)
- Height (h)

---

# 6. Total Surface Area of Cuboid (TSA)

## Definition

The total area of all six faces of a cuboid is called total surface area.

---

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
TSA=2(lb+bh+hl)
}
\]

---

# 7. Lateral Surface Area of Cuboid (LSA)

## Definition

The area of four side faces of a cuboid is called lateral surface area.

---

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
LSA=2h(l+b)
}
\]

---

# 8. Volume of Cuboid

## Definition

The space occupied by a cuboid is called its volume.

---

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
V=l\times b\times h
}
\]

---

# Example

Find volume of cuboid:

Length = 5 cm

Breadth = 4 cm

Height = 3 cm

---

\[
V=5\times4\times3
\]

\[
\boxed{V=60cm^3}
\]

---

# PART C: SURFACE AREA OF CUBE

---

# 9. Cube

## Definition

A cube is a solid figure having six equal square faces.

---

Side length:

\[
a
\]

---

# 10. Total Surface Area of Cube

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
TSA=6a^2
}
\]

---

# 11. Lateral Surface Area of Cube

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
LSA=4a^2
}
\]

---

# 12. Volume of Cube

Formula:

\[
\boxed{
V=a^3
}
\]

---

# Example

Find volume of cube with side:

\[
4cm
\]

Solution:

\[
V=a^3
\]

\[
=4^3
\]

\[
\boxed{64cm^3}
\]

---

# PART D: CAPACITY AND UNIT CONVERSIONS

---

# 13. Volume Units

Common units:

\[
cm^3,\ m^3
\]

---

Conversion:

\[
\boxed{
1m^3=1000000cm^3
}
\]

---

# 14. Capacity

Capacity refers to the amount a container can hold.

---

Units:

- Millilitre (mL)
- Litre (L)

---

Conversion:

\[
\boxed{
1L=1000mL
}
\]

---

Relationship:

\[
\boxed{
1mL=1cm^3
}
\]

# Solved Examples

---

## Example 1

Find semi-perimeter of triangle with sides:

\[
8cm,10cm,12cm
\]

\[
s=\frac{8+10+12}{2}
\]

\[
\boxed{s=15cm}
\]

---

## Example 2

Find TSA of cube with side 5 cm.

\[
TSA=6a^2
\]

\[
=6(5^2)
\]

\[
=150
\]

\[
\boxed{150cm^2}
\]

---

## Example 3

Find volume of cuboid:

\[
l=10cm,\ b=5cm,\ h=2cm
\]

\[
V=10\times5\times2
\]

\[
\boxed{100cm^3}
\]

# Common Mistakes

- Using perimeter instead of semi-perimeter in Heron's formula.
- Confusing surface area and volume units.
- Forgetting cube has equal sides.
- Using square units for volume.
- Using cubic units for area.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Heron's formula finds triangle area when three sides are known.
- Cuboid has rectangular faces with formulas for surface area and volume.
- Cube has equal sides and simpler formulas.
- Capacity is related to volume measurements.
- Correct units are essential in mensuration problems.
