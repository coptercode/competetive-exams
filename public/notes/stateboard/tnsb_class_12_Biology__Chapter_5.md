# PART A: DNA AS GENETIC MATERIAL

---

# 1. Genetic Material

## Definition

The substance that carries hereditary information from parents to offspring is called genetic material.

---

Characteristics of genetic material:

- Should replicate itself.
- Should store information.
- Should express information.
- Should undergo variation.

---

# 2. Search for Genetic Material

---

# Griffith's Transformation Experiment

Scientist:

\[
\boxed{\text{Frederick Griffith}}
\]

Organism used:

_Streptococcus pneumoniae_

---

Experiment:

Two types of bacteria:

## S strain

- Smooth colony.
- Disease causing.

## R strain

- Rough colony.
- Non-pathogenic.

---

Observation:

Heat-killed S bacteria + Live R bacteria

↓

Live S bacteria formed.

---

Conclusion:

A transforming principle transferred genetic information.

---

Two types:

- Adenine (A)

---

# 8. Watson and Crick Model of DNA

Proposed by:

\[
\boxed{\text{Watson and Crick (1953)}}
\]

---

Features:

- Double helical structure.
- Two polynucleotide strands.
- Strands are antiparallel.

---

# 9. Base Pairing

According to Chargaff's rule:

A pairs with T

\[
\boxed{A=T}
\]

G pairs with C

\[
\boxed{G\equiv C}
\]

---

# 10. DNA Structure

Important features:

- Sugar-phosphate backbone.
- Hydrogen bonds between bases.
- Double helix.

---

# 11. RNA

## Full Form

\[
\boxed{\text{Ribonucleic Acid}}
\]

---

Differences from DNA:

- Contains ribose sugar.
- Contains uracil instead of thymine.
- Usually single stranded.

---

---

## Transfer RNA (tRNA)

Function:

Carries amino acids for protein synthesis.

---

## Ribosomal RNA (rRNA)

Function:

# Important Differences

## DNA vs RNA

| DNA                        | RNA                     |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Double stranded            | Usually single stranded |
| Deoxyribose sugar          | Ribose sugar            |
| Contains thymine           | Contains uracil         |
| Stores genetic information | Helps protein synthesis |

---

## Replication vs Transcription

| Replication                 | Transcription              |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| DNA → DNA                   | DNA → RNA                  |
| Produces DNA copy           | Produces RNA               |
| Occurs before cell division | Needed for gene expression |
