# Introduction

Biotechnology is the application of biological organisms, systems and processes for the benefit of humans.

Modern biotechnology mainly involves genetic engineering and recombinant DNA technology.

This chapter covers:

- Genetic engineering
- Recombinant DNA technology
- Tools of biotechnology
- Restriction enzymes
- Cloning vectors
- Gene transfer
- PCR
- Bioreactors
- Downstream processing

---

# PART A: BIOTECHNOLOGY

---

# 1. Biotechnology

## Definition

Biotechnology is the use of living organisms, cells or biological processes to develop useful products and technologies.

---

Applications:

- Medicine production.
- Crop improvement.
- Industrial products.
- Environmental management.

---

# 2. Principles of Biotechnology

Modern biotechnology is based on two main principles:

1. Genetic engineering
2. Maintenance of sterile conditions

---

# PART B: GENETIC ENGINEERING

---

# 3. Genetic Engineering

## Definition

The deliberate modification of an organism's genetic material using biotechnology techniques is called genetic engineering.

---

Also called:

\[
\boxed{\text{Recombinant DNA technology}}
\]

---

# 4. Steps in Genetic Engineering

Major steps:

1. Isolation of desired DNA.
2. Cutting of DNA using enzymes.
3. Joining DNA fragments.
4. Transfer into host organism.
5. Selection of transformed cells.
6. Expression of desired gene.

---

# PART C: RECOMBINANT DNA TECHNOLOGY

---

# 5. Recombinant DNA (rDNA)

## Definition

DNA formed by joining DNA fragments from two different sources is called recombinant DNA.

---

Example:

Human insulin gene inserted into bacterial DNA.

---

# 6. Tools of Recombinant DNA Technology

Major tools:

1. Restriction enzymes
2. DNA ligase
3. Cloning vectors
4. Competent host cells

---

# PART D: RESTRICTION ENZYMES

---

# 7. Restriction Enzymes

## Definition

Enzymes that cut DNA at specific recognition sites are called restriction enzymes.

---

Also called:

\[
\boxed{\text{Molecular scissors}}
\]

---

Discovered by:

\[
\boxed{\text{Werner Arber, Hamilton Smith and Daniel Nathans}}
\]

---

# 8. Restriction Endonucleases

They cut DNA within the strand.

---

Example:

\[
\boxed{\textit{EcoRI}}
\]

---

EcoRI recognizes:

\[
\boxed{GAATTC}
\]

---

# 9. Sticky Ends

## Definition

Single-stranded overhanging ends produced after restriction enzyme cutting are called sticky ends.

---

Importance:

- Help joining DNA fragments.

---

# PART E: DNA LIGASE

---

# 10. DNA Ligase

## Definition

An enzyme that joins DNA fragments by forming phosphodiester bonds is called DNA ligase.

---

Function:

\[
\boxed{\text{Joining DNA fragments}}
\]

---

# PART F: CLONING VECTORS

---

# 11. Vector

## Definition

A DNA molecule used to carry foreign DNA into a host cell is called a vector.

---

Examples:

- Plasmids
- Bacteriophages

---

# 12. Features of an Ideal Vector

A good vector contains:

---

## 1. Origin of Replication (ori)

Function:

- Allows replication of vector DNA.

---

## 2. Selectable Marker

Function:

- Identifies transformed cells.

Example:

Antibiotic resistance genes.

---

## 3. Cloning Site

Function:

- Region where foreign DNA is inserted.

---

# 13. Plasmids

## Definition

Small circular DNA molecules present in bacteria are called plasmids.

---

Importance:

- Used as cloning vectors.

---

# PART G: HOST ORGANISMS

---

# 14. Competent Host Cells

## Definition

Cells capable of receiving foreign DNA are called competent cells.

---

Common host:

\[
\boxed{\textit{E. coli}}
\]

---

# PART H: STEPS OF RECOMBINANT DNA TECHNOLOGY

---

# 15. Isolation of Genetic Material

DNA is isolated from cells.

---

Steps:

1. Cell lysis.
2. Removal of proteins.
3. Purification of DNA.

---

# 16. Cutting of DNA

Restriction enzymes cut:

- Vector DNA.
- Foreign DNA.

---

# 17. Amplification of Gene

The desired DNA fragment is multiplied using:

\[
\boxed{\text{PCR}}
\]

---

# 18. Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)

## Definition

A technique used to make multiple copies of a DNA segment is called PCR.

---

Developed by:

\[
\boxed{\text{Kary Mullis}}
\]

---

# 19. Steps of PCR

---

## 1. Denaturation

DNA strands separate.

Temperature:

\[
\approx 94^\circ C
\]

---

## 2. Annealing

Primers attach to DNA strands.

---

## 3. Extension

DNA polymerase synthesizes new DNA strands.

---

Enzyme used:

\[
\boxed{\text{Taq DNA polymerase}}
\]

---

Source:

\[
\boxed{\textit{Thermus aquaticus}}
\]

---

# 20. Insertion of Recombinant DNA

Foreign DNA is inserted into vector using DNA ligase.

---

# 21. Transformation

## Definition

Introduction of recombinant DNA into host cells is called transformation.

---

# 22. Selection of Transformants

Cells containing desired DNA are selected using selectable markers.

---

# PART I: BIOREACTORS

---

# 23. Bioreactor

## Definition

A vessel used for large-scale production of biological products is called a bioreactor.

---

Functions:

- Provides suitable conditions.
- Maintains temperature and pH.
- Allows mixing and oxygen supply.

---

# 24. Types of Bioreactors

---

## Stirred Tank Bioreactor

Most commonly used.

---

Features:

- Stirrer for mixing.
- Oxygen supply system.
- Temperature control.

---

## Simple Stirred Tank Bioreactor

Contains:

- Agitator.
- Oxygen delivery system.
- Sampling ports.

---

# PART J: DOWNSTREAM PROCESSING

---

# 25. Downstream Processing

## Definition

The processes involved in separation, purification and preparation of final biotechnology products are called downstream processing.

---

Steps:

1. Separation.
2. Purification.
3. Formulation.
4. Quality testing.

---

# Important Differences

## Restriction Enzyme vs DNA Ligase

| Restriction Enzyme | DNA Ligase     |
| ------------------ | -------------- |
| Cuts DNA           | Joins DNA      |
| Molecular scissors | Molecular glue |

---

## Vector vs Host

| Vector              | Host                 |
| ------------------- | -------------------- |
| Carries foreign DNA | Receives foreign DNA |
| Example: Plasmid    | Example: E. coli     |

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing restriction enzymes and ligase.
- Forgetting PCR amplifies DNA.
- Mixing vector and host functions.
- Forgetting plasmids are common cloning vectors.
- Confusing biotechnology and genetic engineering.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Biotechnology uses biological systems for useful applications.
- Genetic engineering modifies DNA intentionally.
- Recombinant DNA technology combines DNA from different sources.
- Restriction enzymes cut DNA and ligase joins fragments.
- Vectors carry foreign genes into host cells.
- PCR amplifies DNA.
- Bioreactors enable large-scale production of biological products.
