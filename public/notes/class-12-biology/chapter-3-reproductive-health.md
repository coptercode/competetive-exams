# Introduction

Reproductive health refers to the total well-being of individuals in all aspects related to reproduction, including physical, emotional and social health.

This chapter covers:

- Need for reproductive health
- Sexually transmitted diseases
- Birth control methods
- Family planning
- Medical termination of pregnancy
- Infertility
- Assisted reproductive technologies

---

# PART A: REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH

---

# 1. Reproductive Health

## Definition

Reproductive health is the ability to have a safe and healthy reproductive life with proper knowledge, care and freedom of choice.

---

# 2. Importance of Reproductive Health

It helps in:

- Preventing sexually transmitted diseases.
- Controlling population growth.
- Ensuring safe motherhood.
- Improving awareness about reproduction.
- Preventing unwanted pregnancies.

---

# 3. Reproductive Health Programmes

Government programmes focus on:

- Sex education.
- Maternal and child health.
- Family planning.
- Prevention of reproductive disorders.

---

# PART B: SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED DISEASES (STDs)

---

# 4. Sexually Transmitted Diseases

## Definition

Diseases transmitted mainly through sexual contact are called sexually transmitted diseases.

---

Also called:

\[
\boxed{\text{Venereal diseases}}
\]

---

# 5. Common STDs

Examples:

| Disease        | Causative Organism |
| -------------- | ------------------ |
| Gonorrhoea     | Bacteria           |
| Syphilis       | Bacteria           |
| AIDS           | HIV virus          |
| Genital herpes | Herpes virus       |
| Genital warts  | HPV                |
| Hepatitis-B    | HBV                |

---

# 6. Symptoms of STDs

Common symptoms:

- Abnormal discharge.
- Pain during urination.
- Genital itching.
- Swelling or sores.
- Fever and weakness.

---

# 7. Prevention of STDs

Methods:

- Avoid unsafe sexual practices.
- Use condoms.
- Maintain personal hygiene.
- Avoid sharing infected needles.
- Early diagnosis and treatment.

---

# PART C: BIRTH CONTROL

---

# 8. Birth Control

## Definition

Methods used to prevent unwanted pregnancy are called contraceptive methods.

---

# 9. Need for Birth Control

Reasons:

- Population control.
- Spacing between children.
- Preventing unwanted pregnancies.
- Protecting maternal health.

---

# PART D: CONTRACEPTIVE METHODS

---

# 10. Natural Methods

Methods based on avoiding intercourse during fertile periods.

---

Examples:

## Periodic Abstinence

Avoiding intercourse during ovulation period.

---

## Withdrawal Method

Removal of penis before ejaculation.

---

## Lactational Amenorrhea

Temporary prevention of ovulation during breastfeeding.

---

# 11. Barrier Methods

Prevent sperm from reaching ovum.

---

Examples:

## Condoms

- Male condom.
- Female condom.

Advantages:

- Prevent pregnancy.
- Protect against STDs.

---

## Diaphragm

Placed over cervix to prevent sperm entry.

---

# 12. Intrauterine Devices (IUDs)

Devices placed inside uterus.

---

Examples:

## Copper-T

Releases copper ions.

Function:

- Prevents fertilization.

---

## Hormone-releasing IUDs

Release hormones to prevent pregnancy.

---

# 13. Oral Contraceptive Pills

Contain hormones:

- Estrogen
- Progesterone

---

Functions:

- Prevent ovulation.
- Prevent implantation.

---

# 14. Injectable and Implant Contraceptives

Release hormones slowly into the body.

---

# 15. Surgical Methods

Permanent methods of contraception.

---

## Vasectomy

Male sterilization.

Procedure:

- Vas deferens is cut and tied.

---

## Tubectomy

Female sterilization.

Procedure:

- Fallopian tubes are cut and tied.

---

# PART E: MEDICAL TERMINATION OF PREGNANCY (MTP)

---

# 16. MTP

## Definition

Intentional termination of pregnancy before full development of foetus is called medical termination of pregnancy.

---

Reasons:

- Unwanted pregnancy.
- Pregnancy due to contraceptive failure.
- Risk to mother's health.
- Genetic abnormalities.

---

# 17. Importance of MTP

- Prevents unsafe abortions.
- Protects maternal health.
- Helps in family planning.

---

# PART F: AMNIOCENTESIS

---

# 18. Amniocentesis

## Definition

A prenatal diagnostic technique used to detect genetic disorders in foetus is called amniocentesis.

---

Procedure:

- Amniotic fluid is collected.
- Foetal cells are analysed.

---

Detects:

- Chromosomal abnormalities.
- Genetic disorders.

---

# Note:

Misuse of amniocentesis for sex determination is illegal.

---

# PART G: INFERTILITY

---

# 19. Infertility

## Definition

The inability to conceive after regular unprotected intercourse is called infertility.

---

Causes:

## Male Factors

- Low sperm count.
- Poor sperm motility.
- Hormonal problems.

---

## Female Factors

- Ovulation problems.
- Blocked fallopian tubes.
- Uterine disorders.

---

# 20. Treatment of Infertility

Methods:

- Medicines.
- Surgery.
- Assisted reproductive technologies.

---

# PART H: ASSISTED REPRODUCTIVE TECHNOLOGIES (ART)

---

# 21. ART

## Definition

Techniques used to help infertile couples achieve pregnancy are called assisted reproductive technologies.

---

Major ART methods:

1. IVF
2. ZIFT
3. GIFT

---

# 22. In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

## Definition

Fusion of sperm and ovum outside the body in laboratory conditions is called IVF.

---

Steps:

1. Collection of eggs.
2. Collection of sperm.
3. Fertilization in laboratory.
4. Embryo transfer to uterus.

---

Commonly called:

\[
\boxed{\text{Test tube baby technique}}
\]

---

# 23. Zygote Intrafallopian Transfer (ZIFT)

## Definition

Transfer of a zygote or early embryo into the fallopian tube is called ZIFT.

---

Process:

Zygote formed by IVF

↓

Transferred into fallopian tube

---

# 24. Gamete Intrafallopian Transfer (GIFT)

## Definition

Transfer of gametes directly into fallopian tube for fertilization is called GIFT.

---

Used when:

- Female can produce healthy eggs.
- Fertilization is possible inside body.

---

# Important Differences

## Vasectomy vs Tubectomy

| Vasectomy              | Tubectomy                  |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Male sterilization     | Female sterilization       |
| Vas deferens cut       | Fallopian tubes cut        |
| Prevents sperm release | Prevents egg-sperm meeting |

---

## IVF vs GIFT

| IVF                               | GIFT                             |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Fertilization occurs outside body | Fertilization occurs inside body |
| Embryo transferred                | Gametes transferred              |

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing STDs and genetic disorders.
- Mixing vasectomy and tubectomy.
- Forgetting condoms prevent STDs.
- Confusing IVF, ZIFT and GIFT.
- Forgetting amniocentesis is a diagnostic technique.

---

# Chapter Summary

- Reproductive health ensures safe and healthy reproductive life.
- STDs can be prevented through safe practices.
- Contraceptive methods help control pregnancy.
- MTP provides safe termination of unwanted pregnancies.
- Infertility can be treated using ART techniques.
- IVF, ZIFT and GIFT help infertile couples achieve pregnancy.
