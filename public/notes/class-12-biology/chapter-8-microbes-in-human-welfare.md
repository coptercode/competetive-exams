# Introduction

Microorganisms are microscopic organisms that play important roles in human life. They are used in food production, industries, medicine, agriculture and environmental management.

This chapter covers:

- Microbes in food processing
- Industrial applications of microbes
- Sewage treatment
- Energy generation
- Bio-control agents
- Bio-fertilisers
- Antibiotics

---

# PART A: MICROBES

---

# 1. Microorganisms

## Definition

Organisms that are too small to be seen with the naked eye are called microorganisms or microbes.

---

Examples:

- Bacteria
- Fungi
- Protozoans
- Algae
- Viruses

# 2. Beneficial Microbes

Microbes are useful in:

- Food production.

# PART D: SEWAGE TREATMENT

---

# 12. Sewage

## Definition

Wastewater containing human waste and other pollutants is called sewage.

---

Treatment occurs in:

1. Primary treatment
2. Secondary treatment

---

# 13. Primary Treatment

## Definition

Physical removal of large particles from sewage is called primary treatment.

---

Processes:

- Filtration.
- Sedimentation.

---

Produces:

\[
\boxed{\text{Primary sludge}}
\]

---

# 14. Secondary Treatment

## Definition

Biological treatment of sewage using microorganisms is called secondary treatment.

---

Microorganisms form:

\[
\boxed{\text{Flocs}}
\]

---

# 15. Activated Sludge

## Definition

The sediment containing microbial biomass formed during sewage treatment is called activated sludge.

---

Function:

- Degrades organic matter.

---

# 16. Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)

## Definition

The amount of oxygen required by microorganisms to decompose organic matter in water is called BOD.

---

High BOD indicates:

\[
\boxed{\text{High pollution}}
\]

---

Chemical substances produced by microorganisms that inhibit or kill other microorganisms are called antibiotics.

---

First discovered antibiotic.

\[

# 29. Production of Antibiotics

Steps:

1. Selection of microorganism.
2. Growth in fermenter.
3. Extraction of antibiotic.
4. Purification.

A vessel used for large-scale production of microbial products is called a fermenter.

---

# PART I: JUDICIOUS USE OF ANTIBIOTICS

---

# 31. Proper Use of Antibiotics

Important rules:

- Use only when prescribed.
- Complete the full course.
- Avoid unnecessary use.

---

# 32. Antibiotic Resistance

## Definition

Ability of microorganisms to survive antibiotic treatment is called antibiotic resistance.

---

Causes:

- Overuse of antibiotics.
- Incorrect dosage.
- Stopping treatment early.

---

# Important Differences

## Primary vs Secondary Sewage Treatment

| Primary Treatment       | Secondary Treatment       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Physical process        | Biological process        |
| Removes solid particles | Uses microorganisms       |
| Produces sludge         | Produces activated sludge |

---

## Bio-fertiliser vs Chemical Fertiliser

| Bio-fertiliser       | Chemical Fertiliser     |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Uses microorganisms  | Uses chemicals          |
| Eco-friendly         | May cause pollution     |
| Improves soil health | May damage soil quality |

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing microbes and pathogens.
- Forgetting yeast is used in fermentation.
- Mixing bio-control agents and bio-fertilisers.
- Forgetting methane is the major component of biogas.
- Ignoring antibiotic resistance.

- Sewage treatment uses microorganisms to remove pollutants.

- Bio-fertilisers improve soil fertility.
